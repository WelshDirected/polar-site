history of chess

25 November 2026

I never imagined a simple board could travel from ancient temples to the digital clouds. The journey of chess is truly brilliant.

Chess began as the Indian game chaturanga about six centuries ago, later spreading to Persia where it became shatranj. Arab conquests carried it into the Muslim world and then into Europe via Spain and Italy. By the 1500s the rules had largely settled into the modern form we know today. The first official World Championship was held in 1886, and the 20th century saw the rise of FIDE and computer dominance after Deep Blue's 1997 victory. Online platforms have turned chess into a global digital pastime.
