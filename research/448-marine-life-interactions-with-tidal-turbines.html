marine life interactions with tidal turbines

26 November 2026

I’m impressed by how engineers blend physics and biology to protect marine life while harvesting tides.

Tidal turbines emit low‑frequency sound and pressure fluctuations that can disturb fish hearing and marine mammal communication. Acoustic modeling helps predict where noise levels exceed species‑specific thresholds. Blade‑strike risk is mitigated by slow‑rotating, larger‑diameter rotors and by placing turbines away from migration routes. Turbine foundations can alter local seabed habitats, sometimes providing artificial reef benefits but also changing sediment flow. Ongoing monitoring using hydrophones and animal tagging is essential to assess real‑world impacts.
