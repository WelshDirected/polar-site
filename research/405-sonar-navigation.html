sonar navigation

25 November 2026

I never imagined sound could map the sea as precisely as the stars guide the ice. Fascinating.

Sonar works by sending out high‑frequency sound pulses and listening for the returning echoes, which reveal the distance and shape of underwater objects. There are three common sonar formats: Searchlight (PPI) sonar uses a narrow rotating beam for a detailed 360° sweep, Sector Scan steps the beam in 45° increments for quicker coverage, and Scanning sonar emits pulses in all directions at once for instant full‑circle detection. The narrow 6° beam of Searchlight sonar is favored on small craft, while Scanning sonar can instantly spot fast‑moving fish such as tuna. Modern sonar is popular on both commercial fishing boats and recreational vessels, extending the age‑old practice of navigating by natural cues into the acoustic realm.
