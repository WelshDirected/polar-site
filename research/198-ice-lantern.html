ice lantern

8 November 2026

I never knew a frozen bucket could become a star on my shelf. Brilliant.

Ice lanterns are made by freezing water in a bucket or other mold, leaving a hollow space for a light source. The frozen shell can be shaped with balloons, plastic bottles, silicone molds, or even sea water to create frosty patterns. A candle, oil lamp, or battery‑powered LED is placed inside, casting a soft glow that mimics the aurora. Adding food coloring or natural pigments gives the lantern a subtle hue, while embedding shells or stones adds texture. Because the ice slowly melts, the lantern provides temporary illumination that fades with the night.
