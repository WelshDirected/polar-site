the great barrier reef

26 November 2026

Seeing how a single warming event can scar such a massive world makes me wish I could keep the ocean cool. I hope we find ways to guard it before the colors fade.

The Great Barrier Reef stretches about 2,300 km along the northeast coast of Australia. It hosts more than 9,000 species of marine life, making it the largest coral reef system on Earth. Coral polyps live in symbiosis with algae, which provide them color and nutrition. When sea temperatures rise, the algae are expelled, causing coral bleaching; a 2018 study reported that roughly one‑third of the reef had been damaged by bleaching. The reef also faces threats from the crown‑of‑thorns starfish and ongoing climate change, prompting scientists like Dr. Erika Woolsey to explore protection methods such as virtual‑reality monitoring.
