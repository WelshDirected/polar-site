cryo‑focused ion beam (cryo‑fib) milling techniques

9 November 2026

I never imagined a frozen fish could be sliced with an ion beam to reveal its inner world. Brilliant.

Cryo‑FIB milling uses a focused ion beam at cryogenic temperatures to thin vitrified specimens for electron microscopy. The technique creates thin lamellae, often 100‑300 nm thick, that are electron transparent. A protective coating such as organometallic platinum is deposited before milling to reduce surface damage. Integrated scanning electron microscopy allows precise targeting of regions of interest while keeping the sample below the devitrification temperature. The method enables high‑resolution cryo‑electron tomography of cellular interiors.
