ai‑based phase reconstruction

10 November 2026

I’m impressed how a single shot can give both phase and fluorescence, making imaging both swift and gentle.

AI-driven dual-mode imaging uses a single-shot Gabor hologram to capture both phase and fluorescence information. A neural network is trained to reconstruct quantitative phase maps from the hologram intensity, bypassing iterative algorithms. This approach yields faster, noise‑robust phase images and enables simultaneous label‑free morphology with fluorescence contrast. The system can operate in real time, useful for live‑cell or dynamic samples. Combining phase and fluorescence expands the kinds of structures that can be observed without staining.
