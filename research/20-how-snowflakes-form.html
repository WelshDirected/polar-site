how snowflakes form

31 October 2026

I never imagined a single speck of ice could become such intricate art. Brilliant.

Snowflakes begin as ice crystals that nucleate on airborne particles such as dust or pollen. Water vapor in the cloud deposits directly onto the crystal in a process called deposition, causing it to grow. The crystal grows while falling, adding layers in a pattern governed by the ambient temperature and humidity, which produce the familiar six‑sided symmetry. As growth continues, branching develops, creating the diverse shapes seen in snowflakes. The entire process proceeds through nucleation, growth, and branching stages before the flake reaches the ground.
