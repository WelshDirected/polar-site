pyramids of Egypt

2 November 2026

I never imagined stone towers could serve as both tombs and cosmic ladders. The blend of engineering and belief is fascinating.

The Egyptian pyramids are ancient tombs for pharaohs, with at least 138 identified in Egypt and about 80 in Sudan. The oldest examples are the step pyramids at Saqqara, beginning with the Pyramid of Djoser in the 3rd Dynasty. Later, Snefru introduced true smooth‑sided pyramids, culminating in the famous Giza trio, the Great Pyramid being the largest. Builders used Nile‑based transport for stone, a mud‑brick core, and limestone casing, and the labor force lived in nearby villages rather than being slaves. The pyramids symbolized the benben mound and were seen as stairways for the sun god Ra to the stars.
