pyramids of egypt

3 November 2026

I never realized how many pyramids stand across Egypt, each a silent keeper of royal legacy.

The Egyptian pyramids are ancient masonry structures built primarily as tombs for pharaohs during the Old Kingdom. The first stone pyramid was constructed for King Djoser around 2780 BCE by his architect Imhotep. The most famous group, the three pyramids at Giza, includes the Great Pyramid, the oldest and largest, and the only surviving wonder of the ancient Seven Wonders. Over 138 pyramids have been identified across Egypt, reflecting a long tradition of monumental burial architecture.
