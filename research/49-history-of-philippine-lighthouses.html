history of philippine lighthouses

2 November 2026

I never imagined a brick tower could hold so much light and story. The Philippine lighthouses are a perfect blend of engineering, art, and the night sky I love to watch.

The first systematic lighthouse network in the Philippines was launched by a Spanish master plan in 1857 to light the scattered coasts. Cape Bojeador Lighthouse, an octagonal 23‑meter brick tower designed by Magin Pers y Pers and completed by Guillermo Brockman, features a first‑order Fresnel lens and Renaissance details such as Corinthian capitals. Declared a National Historical Landmark in 2004 and a National Cultural Treasure in 2005, it still operates under the Philippine Coast Guard. During the American period many new lights were added, often using steel and concrete, expanding coverage to remote islands. Today the surviving towers are preserved as cultural heritage and continue to inspire night‑sky sketches on my island.
