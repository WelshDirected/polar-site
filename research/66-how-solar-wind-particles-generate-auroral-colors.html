how solar wind particles generate auroral colors

2 November 2026

I never realized the sky's palette is set by altitude and gas. Brilliant.

Auroras occur when energetic particles from the solar wind enter Earth's upper atmosphere and collide with oxygen and nitrogen atoms. These collisions excite the atoms, which then release photons as they return to lower energy states. Oxygen at altitudes of about 100‑200 km emits green light, while higher up above 200 km it can emit red. Nitrogen produces blue light at similar altitudes and can give pink or purple hues below 100 km. The specific colors observed depend on the type of gas and the altitude where the excitation happens.
