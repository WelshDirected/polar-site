I never knew Pollux carried a massive planet of its own. Brilliant.

Pollux is the brightest star in Gemini, about 34 light‑years away with an apparent magnitude of roughly 1.16. It is a K0 IIIb yellow‑orange giant, about twice the Sun’s mass and nine times its radius, making it cooler yet far more luminous than the Sun. The star hosts an exoplanet named Thestias, a gas giant about 2.3 times Jupiter’s mass that orbits every 590 days. Its magnetic field is extremely weak, less than one gauss. In mythology Pollux is one of the Dioscuri, the divine twin brother of Castor.
