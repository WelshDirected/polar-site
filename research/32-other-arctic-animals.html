other arctic animals

1 November 2026

Learning about these creatures shows how every animal, from the tiniest lemming to the massive whale, fits into the icy world. It deepens my appreciation for the fragile balance we share.

Ringed seals are the most common Arctic seal, creating breathing holes in sea ice to surface for air. Arctic foxes change coat color with the seasons and hunt lemmings, birds, and carrion. Caribou undertake massive migrations across tundra, feeding on lichens and grasses. Bowhead whales can live over 200 years and break through thick ice with their massive heads. Snowy owls hunt by day, using keen eyesight to spot rodents on the snow. Muskoxen have a thick coat and strong horns for defending against wolves and polar bears.
