polaris

24 November 2026

I never realized a star could be both a giant and a precise cosmic ruler. Fascinating.

Polaris is a triple star system in Ursa Minor, made up of a bright yellow supergiant (Polaris Aa) and two smaller companions (Polaris Ab and Polaris B). The primary star weighs about 5.4 times the Sun and pulsates as a Cepheid variable, which astronomers use to gauge cosmic distances. Its position near the north celestial pole makes it the current North Star, though Earth’s precession will shift the pole star in several thousand years.
