the pyramids of egypt

2 November 2026

I never imagined stone towers could be both tombs and cosmic ladders; the Egyptian pyramids are a brilliant marriage of belief and skill.

Pyramids in Egypt are ancient stone tombs built for pharaohs and their consorts. The earliest monumental pyramid is the step pyramid of Djoser at Saqqara, dating to around 2630‑2610 BC. Over 138 pyramids have been identified in Egypt, with the most famous being the Great Pyramid of Khufu at Giza, the only surviving member of the Seven Wonders. Builders used Nile transport and a temporary harbor on the Giza plateau to move limestone blocks. The Egyptian word for pyramid, “mr”, is linked to the benben, the primeval mound of creation myth. Pyramid design evolved from mastaba graves to step pyramids under Djoser, then to true smooth‑sided pyramids under Snefru and reached its peak in the 4th Dynasty before declining in size and precision.
