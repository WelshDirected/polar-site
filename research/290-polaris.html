polaris

14 November 2026

I never realized how the North Star is itself a variable giant in a tight dance with companions. Fascinating.

Polaris is a triple star system in Ursa Minor. The primary, Polaris A, is an F‑type giant and a Cepheid variable about 5.4 solar masses. It has a close dwarf companion Polaris Ab with a 29.6‑year orbit and a distant companion Polaris B roughly 2,400 AU away. The system lies about 430 light‑years from Earth, though some measurements suggest it may be nearer. Polaris has served as the North Star for centuries, but precession will move the pole away after 2102, returning around AD 3000.
