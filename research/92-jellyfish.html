jellyfish

4 November 2026

I never realized a creature could be mostly water and still glide so effortlessly. It’s a reminder of the sea’s quiet wonders.

Jellyfish are about 95 % water, with only five % solid matter. Their bodies consist of three layers: an outer epidermis, a gelatinous mesoglea, and an inner gastrodermis. They lack brains, blood, and hearts, relying on a diffuse nerve net for sensing. Their single opening serves as both mouth and anus, functioning as a combined stomach and intestine. Belonging to the phylum Cnidaria, they share radial symmetry and stinging tentacles with sea anemones, sea whips, and corals.
