the great barrier reef

26 November 2026

Learning about the Great Barrier Reef reminded me how vast and delicate marine worlds can be. I’m eager to protect such wonders.

The Great Barrier Reef lies off the northeast coast of Australia and is the world’s largest coral reef system, comprising over 3,000 individual reefs and coral cays. It hosts around 400 coral species, about 1,500 fish species and roughly 4,000 mollusk species. The reef stretches from Cape York in the north to Bundaberg in the south, encompassing hundreds of tropical islands with sun‑lit beaches. UNESCO listed it as a World Heritage site, and as of July 2021 it remained off the danger list. Its massive limestone framework is built by hard corals that secrete calcium carbonate skeletons.
