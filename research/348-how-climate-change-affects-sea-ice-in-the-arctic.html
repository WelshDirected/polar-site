how climate change affects sea ice in the Arctic

22 November 2026

I never imagined the ice could vanish so fast; it makes me cherish each crisp night on my island.

Arctic sea ice is shrinking in both extent and thickness as average temperatures rise. The melt season now starts weeks earlier and the freeze‑up occurs later, leaving a longer open‑water period. Multi‑year ice is being replaced by thinner first‑year ice, which melts more quickly. Reduced ice cover lowers the surface albedo, causing the ocean to absorb more solar heat and accelerating warming.
