aurora borealis color spectrum

7 November 2026

I never imagined the night sky could paint with such chemistry. Brilliant.

Green light in the aurora comes from oxygen atoms about 100 to 200 km above Earth. Red auroras are produced by high‑altitude oxygen around 200 km, often seen as a faint glow. Blue and purple colors arise from nitrogen molecules at lower altitudes, below 60 km, with purple being a blend of nitrogen and oxygen emissions. The exact colors depend on the type of gas, the altitude of the collisions, and the energy of the incoming solar particles. During strong solar storms the spectrum broadens, making more vivid and varied hues, and cameras can record brighter colors than the human eye perceives.
