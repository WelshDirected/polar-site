tidal energy harvesting

24 November 2026

I’m fascinated how the gentle push of the tide can power whole villages; it feels like the sea whispering electricity into the night.

Tidal energy converts the kinetic energy of moving water caused by the rise and fall of tides into electricity. The most common systems are tidal stream turbines that rotate with currents and ebb‑flow barrages that trap water behind a dam to release it through turbines. Current projects are mostly demonstrations, still in early development, and often combine shoreline protection with localized power generation. Numerical simulations and experimental tests are used to optimise turbine placement and predict dynamic responses. While the technology is clean and predictable, it remains costly and site‑specific.
