how birds migrate

13 November 2026

I never realized how many birds are tracked worldwide; the scale is astonishing.

The Bird Migration Explorer map shows tracks for 17,658 individual birds from 232 species. The data were gathered from 691 studies and are stored on the Movebank and Motus platforms. Researchers and partners contributed the observations, and the map uses a custom basemap created by Esri. The site also provides ways to contribute new data and contact the Migratory Bird Initiative.
