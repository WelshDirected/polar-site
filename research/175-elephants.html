elephants

7 November 2026

I never imagined such massive creatures could be so tender and socially rich. Their quiet strength makes my cold nights feel a little warmer.

Elephants are the biggest land mammals and include three species: the African savanna, African forest, and Asian elephant. They live in tight matriarchal families where females lead and share knowledge across generations. Their trunks are versatile tools for breathing, drinking, feeding, and social gestures, while their low‑frequency rumbles travel many kilometres for long‑range communication. A pregnancy lasts about 22 months, the longest of any land animal, and calves stay with the herd for years. Major threats are illegal ivory poaching and the loss of natural habitats, which have driven many populations toward endangerment.
