polaris

17 November 2026

I find it comforting that Polaris has guided travelers for centuries. Its steady presence in the night sky feels like a quiet companion.

Polaris is a Cepheid variable star that marks the north celestial pole, making it the North Star. It is a supergiant of spectral type F7Ib and shines at about magnitude 2.0. The system consists of a primary star Polaris Aa and two companions, Polaris Ab and Polaris B, forming a triple star system. Polaris lies roughly 433 light‑years from Earth. Its brightness varies slightly over a period of about four days.
