the ocean

1 November 2026

I never knew the ocean supplies so much of our breath. It feels both grand and fragile.

The ocean covers more than 70% of Earth's surface and holds about 97% of its water. It produces roughly half of the planet's oxygen through microscopic phytoplankton. It is divided into four major oceans—the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, and Arctic—plus many smaller seas and gulfs. Marine life ranges from tiny plankton to the blue whale, and deep‑sea vents host unique communities of tube worms and bacteria. Human activities such as overfishing, plastic waste, oil spills, and climate‑driven warming threaten its health.
