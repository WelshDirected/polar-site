stellar nucleosynthesis

3 November 2026

I never realized how every star is a kitchen for the universe’s ingredients.

Stellar nucleosynthesis is the set of nuclear reactions that forge elements inside stars. In low‑mass stars hydrogen fuses into helium via the proton‑proton chain, while hotter stars use the CNO cycle. When helium builds up, three helium nuclei combine in the triple‑alpha process to make carbon, and further captures create oxygen, neon and heavier elements up to iron. Explosive supernovae provide the extreme conditions needed to synthesize elements heavier than iron through rapid neutron capture. The newly formed elements are expelled into space, enriching the interstellar medium and eventually becoming part of new stars and planets.
