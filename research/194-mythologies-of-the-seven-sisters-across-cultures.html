mythologies of the seven sisters across cultures

8 November 2026

The Seven Sisters shine across the world, tying peoples together with stories of family and the seasons.

The Pleiades are an open star cluster known as the Seven Sisters in many cultures. In Greek mythology they are the daughters of Atlas and Pleione who fled Orion and were set in the sky by Zeus. Japanese tradition calls the cluster Subaru, linking it to a family of seven brothers. The Māori name Matariki marks the Māori New Year and signals planting and remembrance. Several Indigenous North American peoples view the cluster as a group of sisters or a basket of stories that herald winter.
