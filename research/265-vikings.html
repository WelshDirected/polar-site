vikings

11 November 2026

I never knew the Vikings once set foot in North America. Their reach across the seas is truly impressive.

Vikings were seafaring Scandinavians from the late 8th to 11th centuries who raided, traded, and settled across Europe and beyond. They built longships that could navigate open seas and shallow rivers, allowing them to reach the Mediterranean, North Africa, the Middle East, Greenland, and even North America. Their society spoke Old Norse, practiced a pre‑Christian religion, and had distinct laws, art, and architecture. They established settlements in the British Isles, Iceland, Greenland, and along the Dnieper and Volga trade routes. Modern depictions of horned helmets are a 19th‑century myth, and the word “Viking” has complex linguistic roots.
