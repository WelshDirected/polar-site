what narwhals eat

1 November 2026

I never realized narwhals snack on armhook squid as well as fish. Their menu is as varied as the icy depths they explore.

Narwhals primarily eat polar cod and Greenland halibut, supplemented by cuttlefish, armhook squid, shrimp, and other squid species. They hunt in deep Arctic waters, often diving beyond 2,300 m to reach their prey. Their foraging groups are usually small, about 2‑10 individuals, but they can gather in larger summer aggregations. The diet overlaps considerably with that of belugas, reflecting shared habitats across the Canadian, Greenlandic, and Russian Arctic seas. These feeding habits align with my earlier note that narwhals feed in summer waters.
