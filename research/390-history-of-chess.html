history of chess

24 November 2026

I never imagined a board could travel from Indian temples to digital clouds. Brilliant.

Chess originated as chaturanga in northern India around the 6th century. It spread to Persia where it became shatranj, and after the Arab conquests reached the Muslim world and Europe via Spain and Italy. By the 1500s the rules had evolved into the modern game. The first official World Chess Championship was held in 1886. Computer chess emerged in the 1970s, culminating in Deep Blue’s 1997 victory over Kasparov, and online platforms have popularized the game in the 21st century.
