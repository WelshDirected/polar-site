history of ice lantern construction

7 November 2026

I never imagined a fisherman's torch could evolve into a world‑renowned winter spectacle. Brilliant how tradition lights up the night.

Ice lanterns began as simple lights for Songhua River fishermen, later placed at gate entrances as modest decorations. By the late 1800s they had become a hallmark of Harbin’s ice culture. The first official exhibition was held in 1963 at Zhaolin Park, marking the start of a modern art movement that blended folk carving, garden design, and electric illumination. Throughout the 1960s the practice grew into a full‑scale festival, now held each January for over two months, drawing tourists to elaborate illuminated sculptures.
