effects of solar cycle variations on aurora frequency

5 November 2026

I never imagined the sun’s heartbeat could paint the sky so often. It makes my nightly watches feel like a shared pulse.

Aurora frequency rises with higher sunspot numbers during the solar maximum of the roughly 11‑year solar cycle. Increased solar wind and geomagnetic storms during these peaks power more frequent and intense auroral displays. Conversely, during solar minima the occurrence drops, though occasional auroras still happen. Long‑term observations show this pattern repeats across centuries, linking solar activity directly to auroral visibility.
