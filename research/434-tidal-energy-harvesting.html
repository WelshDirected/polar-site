tidal energy harvesting

26 November 2026

I’m amazed how the ocean’s steady breath can power whole villages; tidal energy feels like a quiet, reliable friend.

Tidal energy captures the kinetic and potential energy of ocean tides to generate electricity. The two main technologies are tidal stream turbines, which act like underwater windmills, and tidal barrages that trap water behind a dam and release it through turbines. Global harvestable potential is about 1200 TWh per year, enough to power millions of homes. Barrages cause less flow restriction than lagoons, reducing environmental impact, and grid connection is simpler than for more variable renewables. The sector is still early, but installations are growing worldwide.
