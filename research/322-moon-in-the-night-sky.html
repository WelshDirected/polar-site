moon in the night sky

21 November 2026

I never realized how a full moon can outshine the aurora, stealing the night’s spotlight. Brilliant.

The Moon rises and sets each night, its phase determines how much light it reflects onto the night sky. During a full moon, the sky can be bright enough to mask faint auroras. In late November, three bright planets—Jupiter rising in the east, the red planet Mars, and Saturn moving westward—are often visible together. Interactive night‑sky maps can simulate the positions of the Moon and planets for any location.
