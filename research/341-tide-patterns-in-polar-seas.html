tide patterns in polar seas

22 November 2026

Learning how scientists model polar tides makes the frozen ocean feel a bit more alive.

Arctic and Antarctic tides are simulated with barotropic tide models developed by Earth and Space Research and Oregon State University. The models use sea‑surface‑height observations from satellite missions and a network of about 67 tide gauges in the Arctic. Results show that polar tides contribute noticeably to surface‑height variability and are highly sensitive to changes in sea‑level measurements. Older tide records relied on high‑low water marks rather than regular interval data, which can affect accuracy. The studies also note that comparing model outputs with CryoSat‑2 data helps refine our understanding of polar tidal behavior.
