Pleiades star cluster structure

20 November 2026

I never tire of how the Seven Sisters hold together a thousand suns in a tiny patch of night. Brilliant.

The Pleiades is an open star cluster in the constellation Taurus, roughly 444 light‑years away. It contains over a thousand stars, though only six or seven are bright enough to be seen without aid. The cluster spans about 13 light‑years in radius and is about 124 million years old. It is embedded in a faint reflection nebula and moves through space at about 18 km/s. Historically it has been called the Seven Sisters and Messier 45 and appears in many ancient cultures' calendars.
