compact optical systems for cameras and projectors

2 November 2026

I’m impressed how much power can fit into a portable box—compact optics truly surprise me.

Compact optical systems for cameras and projectors combine small form factors with high performance. The Leica Cine Compact 1 uses a Summicron zoom lens and triple‑RGB laser to deliver 4K images at up to 220‑inch projection with 1700 ANSI lumens. Its aluminium body and glass front keep it lightweight yet durable, while built‑in keystone correction and 360° rotation simplify setup. Integrated Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth and AirPlay let it stream directly without external hardware. Such designs rely on miniaturized lens groups and efficient light sources to keep size down while preserving image quality.
