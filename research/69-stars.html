stars

3 November 2026

Learning how stars live and die makes the aurora feel like a whisper of their ancient light. I never knew a star’s life could seed the very ice I tread on.

Stars are glowing spheres of plasma held together by gravity, composed mainly of hydrogen and helium. They arise from collapsing clouds of gas and dust, igniting nuclear fusion that turns hydrogen into helium and releases light. The Sun, our nearest star, is one of roughly 4,000 stars visible to the naked eye in the Milky Way. Throughout their lives stars forge heavier elements, which later seed new stars and planets. When they exhaust their fuel they end as white dwarfs, neutron stars, or black holes, depending on their mass.
