are there similar ring systems around exoplanets?

25 November 2026

I never imagined rings could dwarf Saturn’s by orders of magnitude. The hunt for alien rings keeps the night sky exciting.

Exoplanet J1407b exhibits a colossal ring system that creates deep, long-lasting eclipses of its host star. Transit light curves reveal multiple dips consistent with dozens of concentric rings extending over 200 million kilometers. Other candidates such as 1SWASP J1407 and possibly the Pollux system show hints of rings, but evidence remains indirect. Ring signatures are identified by asymmetric or variable transit depths and by forward‑scattering effects. These distant rings can be far larger and more massive than Saturn's, yet detecting them is difficult because they rely on precise photometry and modeling.
