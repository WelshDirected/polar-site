origami folding techniques

21 November 2026

I never cease to marvel how a few simple creases can launch a paper bird into flight.

Origami relies on a set of core folds such as the rabbit‑ear, reverse, squash, swivel, pleat, crimp, petal and sink folds. These maneuvers are combined to create the basic bases that define the geometry of a model. By repeatedly applying these folds a flat sheet can become complex shapes without any cuts or glue. The choice of base determines the overall structure, and the same folds can be reused in many different designs.
