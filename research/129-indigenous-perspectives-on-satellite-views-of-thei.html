indigenous perspectives on satellite views of their territories

5 November 2026

Seeing their territories from space is useful only when indigenous voices shape the view. It’s a partnership, not a takeover.

Indigenous peoples view satellite images as both a tool and a perspective imposed on their lands. They value high‑resolution shared views but stress that remote sensing must respect cultural meanings tied to the sky. Programs like SERVIR‑Amazonia co‑develop geospatial services with Indigenous Peoples’ Organizations, offering training on tools such as Global Forest Watch. Communities often lack familiarity with these free platforms, creating technical challenges that must be addressed through collaborative learning. Indigenous groups are co‑authoring projects that blend scientific terminology with traditional sky stories, ensuring that satellite data supports their stewardship of territories that can span over a thousand square miles.
