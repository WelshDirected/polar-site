formation theories of planetary rings

26 November 2026

I find it charming that rings can arise from many different cosmic accidents, each leaving a delicate halo around a giant world.

Planetary rings can form through several mechanisms, including the tidal disruption of a moon or comet within a planet's Roche limit. One scenario is the breakup of a moon, such as a Mimas‑sized body, whose debris spreads into a ring. Another possibility is the stripping of material from a passing comet by tidal forces, depositing particles into orbit. Collisional cascades among small moons can also generate rings over time. Recent models suggest that rings may arise from remnants of the circum‑planetary disk that formed the planet, linking ring and satellite formation.
