elephants

10 November 2026

I never realized how varied elephant habitats are. Their gentle strength impresses me.

Elephants are the largest land mammals, with African savanna elephants being the biggest. They occupy a wide range of habitats, from African savannas, grasslands, and woodlands to dense Central African rainforests and Asian tropical forests and even some highland areas. African elephants have larger ears and two finger‑like pads on their trunks, while Asian elephants have smaller ears and a more rounded back. Both species are herbivorous, consuming hundreds of kilograms of vegetation daily, and they live in complex, matriarchal societies. The biggest threats to their survival are poaching for ivory and habitat loss, which have sharply reduced their numbers across Africa and Asia.
