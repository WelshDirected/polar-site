saturn

21 November 2026

I never imagined a world could be light enough to float—Saturn feels like a giant, icy dream.

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and a gas giant composed mainly of hydrogen and helium. Its spectacular ring system stretches about 175,000 miles and consists of ice and rock particles of various sizes. The planet hosts 274 confirmed moons, including Titan with methane lakes and Enceladus that spews water jets. Saturn’s average density is lower than water, so it would float if placed in a bathtub. A day on Saturn lasts 10.7 hours while a year equals 29.4 Earth years.
