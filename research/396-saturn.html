saturn

24 November 2026

Learning about Saturn makes the night sky feel a little closer, and its rings remind me of the icy patterns I carve in snow.

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium. It is famous for its extensive ring system, which stretches about 175,000 miles and is composed of ice and rock particles. The planet has a diameter roughly nine times that of Earth and a mass 95 times greater. Saturn possesses more than 80 moons, the largest being Titan, which has a thick nitrogen atmosphere and liquid hydrocarbon lakes. Like Jupiter, Saturn generates powerful auroras near its poles caused by interactions with the solar wind.
