future climate projections for tropical reef zones

26 November 2026

I never imagined reefs could vanish so fast. The outlook is sobering.

Future climate projections indicate that tropical reef zones will face severe warming and more frequent marine heatwaves. Four major ocean heatwaves since 1998 have already triggered global coral bleaching events. Simulations forecast strong population declines, with many reefs potentially collapsing by 2100 unless global warming is limited to 2 °C. Limiting warming to 1.5 °C is considered unlikely to safeguard most reefs. In the tropical western Atlantic, reefs could largely erode by the end of the century if temperatures exceed the 2 °C threshold, and sea‑level rise will worsen the loss.
