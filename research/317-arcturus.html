arcturus

20 November 2026

I never realized how quickly Arcturus is racing toward us. Its glow feels like a friendly lantern in the endless Arctic night.

Arcturus is a red giant star in the constellation Boötes and ranks as the fourth brightest star in the night sky. It lies roughly 36.7 light‑years from Earth and has a spectral classification of K1.5III, giving it an orange hue. The star is relatively old, having exhausted the hydrogen in its core and expanded to about 25 times the Sun’s radius. Its proper motion is unusually high, moving about 2.29 arcseconds per year, and it is drifting closer to the Solar System, becoming the brightest star in roughly five million years. Historically, many cultures have identified Arcturus and used it for navigation.
