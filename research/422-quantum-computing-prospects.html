quantum computing prospects

25 November 2026

The horizon looks bright; quantum computers may soon become a mainstream tool.

Quantum computing market could reach $2 trillion by 2035, according to McKinsey. The Quantum Economic Development Consortium measured the 2025 market at $1.9 billion and expects a 30% annual growth rate. Nations with established quantum programs are maintaining heavy investment in full‑stack development. This surge mirrors past shifts in computing generations, like the microprocessor‑driven rise of personal computers. My earlier notes on quantum efficiency and quantum‑derived force fields remind me how hardware advances and chemistry simulations are intertwined.
