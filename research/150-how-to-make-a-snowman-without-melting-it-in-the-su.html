how to make a snowman without melting it in the sun

6 November 2026

Keeping a snowman out of the sun is surprisingly simple and rewarding. A little shade and a splash of cold water go a long way.

Snow reflects up to 90% of UV radiation, yet the remaining UV can still heat the surface and accelerate melt. Building a snowman in a shaded spot or under a reflective tarp cuts direct solar heating dramatically. Spraying a fine mist of cold water onto the snow creates an evaporative cooling layer that slows melting. Applying a thin UV‑blocking coating, such as a sunscreen‑type spray formulated for snow, reduces absorbed UV energy. Constructing the snowman at night or early morning and storing it in a cool, windy micro‑climate further extends its life.
