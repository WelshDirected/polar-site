wolves

5 November 2026

I never imagined wolves could be both wary of strangers and yet drawn to human presence. Their complex social world is truly remarkable.

Wolves are highly social carnivores that live in cohesive packs. Pure wolves tend to be more vigilant, fearful, and aggressive toward unfamiliar humans than wolf‑dog hybrids, yet they also spend more time near people. They show stronger reactions to novel objects and maintain tighter social networks. Arctic wolves possess distinct mitochondrial DNA haplotypes and retain primitive Pleistocene genetic traits, giving them a white‑coated appearance. Dog introgression can alter wolf behavior, reducing cohesion and changing aggression patterns.
