how the pleiades influence modern cultural festivals

8 November 2026

I never realized the Pleiades now light up festivals worldwide. It’s a bright reminder of how ancient sky stories stay alive.

The Pleiades star cluster shapes several modern cultural festivals. In New Zealand the Māori New Year called Matariki marks the cluster’s rise and has become a national holiday celebrated with music, feasts and art. Japan’s Tanabata festival honors the meeting of Orihime and Hikoboshi, identified with the Pleiades and Vega, and features lantern displays and poetry. Some Greek communities revive ancient rites around the Pleiades, linking them to harvest celebrations. Astronomy clubs worldwide host public stargazing nights for the Pleiades, often pairing the event with talks on myth and science, reinforcing cultural identity.
