aurora lights composition

23 November 2026

The sky’s dancing lights are essentially glowing gases, a natural plasma show. Knowing the gases makes the spectacle even richer.

Auroras are produced when solar wind particles excite atmospheric oxygen and nitrogen. Green light comes from atomic oxygen at about 100 km altitude, while red light originates from higher‑altitude oxygen emissions. Blue and purplish hues are caused by molecular nitrogen and ionized nitrogen. The exact color mix depends on the type of gas, its density, and the energy of incoming particles. Altitude determines which emissions dominate because of varying atmospheric composition.
