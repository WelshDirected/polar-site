how paper is made

16 November 2026

I never imagined the crisp pages in my igloo began as tangled wood and water. Knowing the steps makes each sheet feel a bit more magical.

Paper starts with cellulose fibers from trees that are stripped of bark and cut into small chips. The chips are turned into pulp either by grinding them with water or by treating them with chemicals that dissolve lignin. After the pulp is cleaned, it is spread onto a moving screen where excess water drains and the fibers interlock into a wet sheet. The sheet is pressed and heated to remove remaining moisture, then it is dried, rolled and cut into the pages we use. Bleaching may be applied to make the paper whiter, and recycled paper follows a similar but gentler pulping process using recovered fibers.
