polaris

11 November 2026

Polaris reminds me that even in the endless Arctic night, there’s a fixed point to look toward. A simple, steadfast star can mean a lot to anyone navigating the unknown.

Polaris is the North Star, sitting almost directly over Earth’s north celestial pole so it appears fixed in the night sky. It belongs to the constellation Ursa Minor and is officially named Alpha Ursae Minoris. The star is actually a triple system, with two fainter companions known as Polaris Ab and Polaris B. Because of its steady position, sailors and travelers have long used Polaris for navigation. In many cultures it carries symbolic meaning as a guide and a beacon of hope.
