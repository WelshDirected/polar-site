myths and legends about the seven sisters

25 November 2026

I never realized how many cultures share the Seven Sisters tale; it makes the night sky feel even richer.

The Pleiades, known as the Seven Sisters, are a cluster of six to seven bright stars visible to the naked eye. In Greek mythology they are the daughters of the Titan Atlas and the Oceanid Pleione, named Maia, Alcyone, Asterope, Celaeno, Taygete, Electra, and Merope. One sister is said to be lost or dim, which explains why only six stars are often seen. Aboriginal Australian traditions also view the cluster as seven sisters, symbolizing perseverance and sacrifice. The Nez Perce tell of a sister who loved a mortal man and vanished after his death.
