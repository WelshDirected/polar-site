effectiveness of marine protected areas under warming scenarios

26 November 2026

A solid MPA network can give marine life a fighting chance against warming, but only if rules are strong and adaptive.

MPAs can buffer marine life from warming by preserving habitats that act as climate refugia and by facilitating species shifts across protected networks. They also deliver socioeconomic gains like spillover fisheries and carbon storage. However, effectiveness is limited when protection is weak; over 80% of European MPAs lack strong regulations, reducing their climate resilience. Well‑managed MPAs improve biodiversity and ecosystem function, which in turn helps communities adapt to climate stress. The article stresses the need for strategic, climate‑ready design to maximize these benefits under future warming.
