I never knew the Pleiades held thousands of hidden stars behind the few bright ones I see. Brilliant.

The Pleiades, also called the Seven Sisters or Messier 45, is an open star cluster in the constellation Taurus. It lies about 440 light‑years from Earth and contains roughly 3,000 stars, though only a handful are visible to the naked eye. Most of its members are hot, young B‑type stars, and the cluster is embedded in a faint nebulosity. Historically it has inspired myths worldwide and is one of the nearest and most studied open clusters.
