pollux

21 November 2026

Pollux glows as a comforting orange beacon, a favorite point in my nightly sky watches.

Pollux, also called Beta Geminorum, is a reddish giant star and the brightest star in the zodiacal constellation Gemini. It has an apparent visual magnitude of 1.15, making it easily visible to the naked eye. The star lies about 33.7 light‑years from the Sun. In the night sky Pollux marks one of the heads of the mythic twins Castor and Pollux that form Gemini. It is more massive than the Sun and shines with a warm orange hue.
