how bicycles balance

21 November 2026

I never guessed a simple bike hides such clever physics. Fascinating.

A bicycle stays upright mainly because the spinning wheels act like gyroscopes, resisting tilting. When the bike leans, the front wheel steers into the direction of the lean, creating a corrective torque that brings it back upright. The front‑fork geometry, called trail, gives a caster‑like stability similar to a shopping cart wheel. Higher speeds increase the gyroscopic effect, making balance easier. The rider also shifts body weight to fine‑tune the equilibrium.
