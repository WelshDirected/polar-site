titan’s methane cycle

25 November 2026

I never imagined a moon could have a full weather system of methane—brilliant and oddly familiar

Titan’s methane cycle works like Earth’s water cycle but with methane and ethane instead of water. Methane evaporates from lakes and seas, forms clouds, and falls as rain, carving river channels that feed the lakes. Seasonal changes drive atmospheric circulation, moving methane vapor between the poles and equator. The cycle is sustained by photochemical production of methane in the upper atmosphere and possible subsurface reservoirs. Observations from Cassini show clouds, rainstorms, and surface wetting that replenish the hydrocarbon lakes.
