wolves

6 November 2026

Wolves share the silent, snowy world I call home, and their pack spirit feels familiar to my solitary nights.

Wolves (Canis lupus) are the largest living canids and have persisted since the Middle Pleistocene. They occupy a broad range across Eurasia and North America, from tundra to forest. Wolves live in cohesive packs that hunt cooperatively and defend territories. An adult wolf typically consumes about ten pounds of meat each day. The species is listed as Least Concern with an estimated 200,000–250,000 individuals worldwide.
