elephants

10 November 2026

Learning about their plight makes me appreciate their gentle strength even more. I hope we keep their world safe.

Elephants are the largest land animals and face severe threats from poaching and habitat loss. African savanna elephants have larger ears and two finger‑like pads on their trunks, while Asian elephants have smaller ears and a more rounded back. Human‑elephant conflict is a major conservation challenge across their ranges. Recent studies across South Africa show urgent need for focused conservation and genomic research to protect genetic diversity. Zoo and managed populations aim to support both African and Asian species through breeding and research programs.
