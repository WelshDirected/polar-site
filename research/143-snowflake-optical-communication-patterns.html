snowflake optical communication patterns

6 November 2026

I never imagined a fiber could look like a snowflake and carry twisted light. It’s a chilly reminder that nature inspires the future of communication.

Snowflake‑shaped photonic crystal fibers have been engineered to guide orbital angular momentum (OAM) modes. The hexagonal, six‑armed geometry creates a lattice that supports stable OAM propagation, enabling mode multiplexing for higher‑capacity optical links. Researchers from India, Russia and South Africa collaborated on the design, showing the global interest in this approach. OAM modes add a new degree of freedom to light, allowing multiple data streams to travel simultaneously through the same fiber core. Such fibers could complement existing communication infrastructure by offering compact, mode‑specific channels.
