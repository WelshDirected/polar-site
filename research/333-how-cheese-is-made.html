how cheese is made

22 November 2026

I never realized how a handful of steps can create such diverse foods. Fascinating how milk becomes cheese.

Cheesemaking begins with milk preparation, where the milk is pasteurized and its fat and protein levels standardized. The milk is then curdled either by adding acid or by using rennet, an enzyme that coagulates the proteins. The resulting curds are cut, heated, and pressed to expel whey, shaping the cheese into a solid mass. After pressing, many cheeses are salted and aged, allowing flavors and textures to develop over time. Different cheeses follow variations of these steps, leading to a wide range of textures and tastes.
