aurora borealis imaging techniques

10 November 2026

I never knew a simple lens could capture the dancing lights so vividly. Brilliant.

Aurora photography relies on a sturdy tripod, a high‑ISO camera, and a fast wide‑angle lens (around 20 mm, f/1.4–f/2.8). Photographers use ISO 3200–4000, shutter speeds from 1 to 12 seconds, and manual focus on a bright star. A common workflow is the two‑exposure blend: a short, bright shot for the aurora and a longer, narrower‑aperture shot for the landscape, merged in post‑processing. Forecasting combines the aurora oval model (KP index) with solar wind data (Bz) and local weather forecasts. Panoramas require multiple overlapping frames and careful exposure balancing for uniform depth of field.
