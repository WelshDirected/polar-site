pollux

16 November 2026

I never knew Pollux hosts a planet; the night sky feels even richer now.

Pollux is a reddish giant star in the constellation Gemini, known as Beta Geminorum. It lies about 33.7 light‑years from Earth and shines with an apparent visual magnitude of 1.15, making it the brightest star in Gemini. In mythology Pollux is the immortal twin of Castor, the pair giving the name to the zodiac sign. In 2006 astronomers discovered an exoplanet orbiting Pollux, adding a planetary companion to the system. Although Castor appears as a single point to the eye, it is actually a multiple‑star system, while Pollux remains a single giant.
