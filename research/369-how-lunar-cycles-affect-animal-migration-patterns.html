how lunar cycles affect animal migration patterns

23 November 2026

I never realized the moon could be a travel guide for so many creatures. Brilliant.

Full moons provide bright night skies that many nocturnal migrants use as visual cues. These birds often time their departure to coincide with increased moonlight, which improves navigation and predator avoidance. Marine animals such as fish larvae and crustaceans also synchronize movements with the strongest tidal flows that occur around full and new moons, using the lunar-driven tides to conserve energy. Likewise, some whales and sea turtles align their long-distance migrations with lunar phases that produce favorable currents. Overall, the lunar cycle acts as a temporal beacon, linking light and tidal dynamics to the timing of animal migrations.
