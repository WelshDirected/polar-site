how birds migrate

16 November 2026

I never imagined birds could be so meticulously followed; it’s awe‑inspiring.

Bird migration involves millions of individuals traveling thousands of kilometers between breeding and wintering grounds. Modern tracking projects have recorded over 17,000 birds from more than 200 species, revealing routes across continents. Data come from researchers, citizen scientists, and satellite tags, which together map seasonal movements. Many species follow flyways that align with wind patterns and food availability. Climate change is shifting timing and routes, making long‑term monitoring essential.
