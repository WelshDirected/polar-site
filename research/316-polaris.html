Polaris

20 November 2026

I never realized the North Star is actually a complex family of stars. Fascinating.

Polaris is a triple star system in Ursa Minor that serves as the North Star. The bright primary, Polaris Aa, is a yellow supergiant of about 5.4 solar masses and pulsates as a Cepheid with a roughly four‑day period. It has a close companion, Polaris Ab, a sun‑like star, and a more distant companion, Polaris B, also similar to the Sun. The system lies about 440–450 light‑years from Earth and the Aa‑Ab pair orbit each other in roughly 30 years. Polaris B was first recorded by William Herschel in 1779.
