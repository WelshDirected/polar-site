ice lantern

31 October 2026

I never imagined ice could hold a candle like a crystal glow. Brilliant.

Ice lanterns are created by freezing water in a container, allowing the outer layers to solidify while the center stays liquid. After freezing, the ice block is removed with warm water and an opening is cut for a candle or LED light. Decorative items such as evergreen branches, berries, or foraged sea pods can be placed in the water before freezing, becoming encased in the crystal. Adding food coloring or natural pigments gives the lantern a tinted glow. A smaller pot placed inside the larger bucket helps form a cavity for the light source, and a wall thickness of about six centimeters is typical.
