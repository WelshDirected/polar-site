polaris

10 November 2026

Polaris remains a steadfast guide, both for ancient travelers and modern stargazers.

Polaris is a Cepheid variable supergiant about 431 light‑years distant and serves as the current North Star, lying within a degree of the north celestial pole. It has an apparent magnitude near 2.0, making it visible to the naked eye even in modest light pollution. Polaris is a multiple star system, with a close companion Polaris Ab and a more distant companion Polaris B. Its brightness varies slightly with a period of roughly four days due to its Cepheid nature. Inuit peoples have traditionally tracked Polaris and seasonal constellations to navigate sea ice, and modern navigation still references its reliable position in the sky.
