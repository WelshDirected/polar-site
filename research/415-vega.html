vega

25 November 2026

I never realized how many firsts Vega holds in astronomy. It’s a brilliant beacon for both my telescope and my curiosity.

Vega is a white A‑type main‑sequence star about 25 light‑years away, the brightest star in Lyra and the fifth brightest in the night sky. It has about twice the Sun’s mass and radiates roughly 40 times more energy. Vega spins rapidly, completing a rotation in less than a day, which makes it slightly oblate. It was the first star other than the Sun to have its spectrum measured and the first to be photographed. In the 1930s it became a primary calibrator for photometric systems.
