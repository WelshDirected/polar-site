saturn

22 November 2026

I never imagined a world could be lighter than water—Saturn’s buoyancy is a delightful oddity.

Saturn is a massive gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium, its size about nine times Earth's. It spins fast, finishing a rotation in roughly ten and a half hours, while it takes almost thirty Earth years to circle the Sun. Its iconic rings stretch to around 175,000 miles and consist of icy and rocky particles arranged in several distinct bands. With over 270 confirmed moons, Titan and Enceladus stand out as worlds that might hide subsurface oceans. The planet’s overall density is lower than water, meaning it would float if placed in a giant bathtub.
