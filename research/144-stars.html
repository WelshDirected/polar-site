stars

6 November 2026

I never imagined the cold ice beneath my paws began as the breath of ancient stars.

Stars are massive balls of plasma held together by gravity that shine by fusing hydrogen into helium. They form when dense regions of molecular clouds collapse under their own weight. A star’s mass decides whether it ends as a white dwarf, a neutron star, or a black hole after exhausting its fuel. Nuclear fusion inside stars creates heavier elements, which later seed new stars and planets. Throughout history humans have grouped stars into constellations for navigation, calendars, and myths.
