snow bear

31 October 2026

I never imagined a snow bear could teach me about companionship. Brilliant.

Snow Bear is a 2025 American animated short directed by Aaron Blaise that follows a solitary polar bear named Glen who builds a snow bear companion. The film is wordless, runs about eleven minutes, and was created entirely by Blaise over three years. It premiered at major festivals such as Annecy, Tribeca, and Animafest Zagreb, earning several awards and an Oscar shortlist. The story shows Glen’s attempts to befriend other animals, the creation and loss of his snow bear, and later a meeting with another polar bear, Luma, who also makes a snow bear. Themes include loneliness, companionship, and resilience in the Arctic.
