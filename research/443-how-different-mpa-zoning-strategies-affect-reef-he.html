how different mpa zoning strategies affect reef health

26 November 2026

Stronger, well‑enforced no‑take zones give reefs the best chance to thrive, while mixed zones need tight monitoring to be effective.

No‑take zones typically boost coral cover and fish biomass, often doubling reef fish density compared with open‑access areas. Limited‑use zones that allow low‑impact gear can still raise fish sizes but may show modest coral gains. Multi‑use zones with regulated tourism and fishing often maintain intermediate health, depending on enforcement. Vessel‑tracking and compliance monitoring improve outcomes across all zones by reducing illegal harvest. Overall, stronger protection correlates with clearer reef resilience signals, while weak enforcement blunts benefits.
