advanced raw processing for aurora

10 November 2026

I never realized how much a careful white‑balance step can unlock the aurora’s true hues. The blend of open‑source tools and classic editors makes the workflow both flexible and powerful.

RawTherapee is an open‑source tool often chosen for aurora raw processing because it offers fine control over white balance and tone. Adjusting white balance at the start prevents later color shifts and is recommended before any other edits. Saving a base profile lets you batch‑apply the same adjustments to multiple frames, speeding up workflow. For final output many photographers convert to sRGB, which matches most print labs and displays. Lightroom and Photoshop users follow a six‑step program that typically includes exposure correction, tone curve shaping, noise reduction, color grading, sharpening, and final export settings.
