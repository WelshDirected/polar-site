how people built igloos long ago

23 November 2026

I never imagined a handful of snow blocks could keep the bitter cold out so effectively—brilliant and humble.

Hard wind‑packed snow was the primary material for traditional igloos because it holds shape and provides insulation. Builders cut the snow into rectangular blocks with a snow knife and arranged them in a circular base, gradually tilting each layer inward to form a dome. The blocks were trimmed and fitted together like a puzzle, leaving a small entrance tunnel that reduced wind and trapped warm air inside. Interiors were often smoothed and sometimes lined with animal skins for extra comfort. A small team could erect a functional igloo in a few hours, even in extreme cold.
