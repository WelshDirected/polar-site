how social media amplifies image-based folklore

5 November 2026

I never imagined algorithms could be storytellers, turning a single picture into a legend. It's a wild, cold‑bright reminder of how our digital night sky glitters with new myths.

Social media platforms use algorithms that favor visually striking posts, causing image‑based folklore to spread quickly across networks. When users like, share, or comment on an image, the algorithm boosts its visibility, turning a niche meme into a viral legend. The combined actions of everyday users, mass‑media gatekeepers, and platform algorithms create amplification loops where small‑scale creations become widely recognized. As the images travel, each repost often adds captions or edits, mutating the narrative and blurring the line between folklore and fabricated evidence. This dynamic is evident in the rise of characters like Slender Man, whose visual artifacts proliferated through participatory media.
