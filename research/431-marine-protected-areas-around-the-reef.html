marine protected areas around the reef

26 November 2026

I’m glad MPAs give reefs a fighting chance against warming waters. It’s reassuring to see global cooperation shaping those safe zones.

Marine protected areas around coral reefs help keep fish communities stable as ocean temperatures rise. By limiting fishing and other disturbances, they boost biodiversity and allow reefs to recover from bleaching events. Even though MPAs cover a tiny slice of the sea, they protect a disproportionate amount of reef habitat and the species that depend on it. The UNESCO World Heritage Marine Programme links managers of dozens of these areas, fostering shared learning. Overall, MPAs act as safe havens that increase reef resilience to climate stress.
