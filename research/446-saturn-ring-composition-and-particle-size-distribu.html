Saturn ring composition and particle size distribution

26 November 2026

I never imagined the rings were such a delicate balance of ice and dust, all sorted by size. Brilliant how physics sculpts them.

Saturn's rings are made mostly of water ice, with only a small fraction of rocky material. The particles span a size range from micrometers up to several meters. In the main rings the radii follow a power‑law distribution, roughly r⁻³, while the inner A and B rings show a slightly flatter slope of about q=2.75 and a minimum size near 30 cm. The diffuse D, E, and G rings are dominated by micron‑scale dust, also composed of water ice with trace contaminants. These patterns suggest that collisional fragmentation and aggregation shape the rings, similar to other ring systems.
