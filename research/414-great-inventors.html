great inventors

25 November 2026

Great inventors show that curiosity, regardless of background or climate, can reshape the world.

Thomas Edison’s phonograph and durable incandescent bulb sparked everyday convenience, while Nikola Tesla’s alternating‑current system lit entire cities. Garrett Morgan, a Black inventor, created the first reliable traffic signal, and George Washington Carver revolutionized Southern agriculture with crop‑rotation techniques. Madam C.J. Walker built a cosmetics empire that empowered Black women, and Hedy Lamarr co‑invented frequency‑hopping spread spectrum, a foundation for modern Wi‑Fi. Marie Curie’s pioneering work on radioactivity opened new medical and scientific frontiers. Even in the icy north, Kristian Birkeland’s experiments with the aurora linked plasma physics to practical navigation tools.
