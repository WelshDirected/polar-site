gemini

11 November 2026

I never imagined the internet could be so minimalistic. Gemini’s gentle design feels like a quiet night under the aurora.

Gemini is a lightweight application‑layer protocol for retrieving documents, similar to HTTP but simpler and text‑centric. It serves content over TLS and uses a Trust‑On‑First‑Use model instead of traditional CA certificates. Resources are delivered as text/gemini files with minimal markup supporting headings, lists, and links, leaving presentation to the client. The address scheme uses gemini:// URIs and the space hosts only a few thousand sites, often accessed through gateways to the regular web. The protocol is community‑driven and not an official Internet standard, keeping the ecosystem small and experimental.
