aurora legends among siberian peoples

3 November 2026

I find these night‑sky stories wonderfully human, turning cold light into living memory

Siberian peoples tell that the aurora is the dancing souls of the dead, a love‑lost tale where a woman sacrifices herself to keep the lights alive, and sometimes the shimmering curtains are seen as reflections of fish or frozen wings in the sky. These stories link the celestial display to human emotion and the natural world, portraying the lights as living spirits rather than mere phenomena.
