aurora‑friendly night lighting

25 November 2026

A soft amber glow lets the aurora dance unhindered—perfect for my midnight watches on the island.

Aurora‑friendly night lighting uses low‑intensity, warm‑colored light to minimise interference with natural aurora displays. It avoids blue and white LEDs, which scatter more in the atmosphere and mask green and red auroral hues. Shielded, directional fixtures point light downwards, reducing skyglow and preserving dark skies. Adjustable timers and motion sensors keep illumination brief and only when needed. Using amber LEDs with a colour temperature around 2000‑2500 K provides enough safety while keeping the aurora visible.
