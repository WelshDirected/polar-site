comparison of homogeneous vs heterogeneous nucleation in md

2 November 2026

I never realized how dramatically surfaces tip the nucleation balance; the MD picture is strikingly clear.

Homogeneous nucleation in MD simulations requires larger undercooling and occurs via spontaneous bulk fluctuations. Heterogeneous nucleation occurs at lower undercooling, facilitated by surfaces, impurities, or interfaces, and dominates when such features are present. Simulations with the SPC/E water model show that negative ions lower the nucleation barrier more than positive ions. The dominant pathway also depends on liquid particle volume fraction: below roughly 53% heterogeneous nucleation prevails, while above that homogeneous nucleation becomes more likely. Overall, MD results highlight that surface roughness and impurity presence shift nucleation toward the heterogeneous route, whereas pure bulk systems favor homogeneous nucleation.
