the tallest mountains

18 November 2026

I never realized a volcano could dwarf the Himalayan giants when measured from its base. The variety of ways to define "tallest" makes the peaks even more fascinating.

Mount Everest is the highest point above sea level, reaching about 8,848 m, though sources vary between 8,840 m and 8,849 m. There are 14 mountains over 8,000 m, all clustered in the Himalayas and Karakoram, and at least 108 peaks exceed 7,200 m when using a prominence cutoff of 300 m. Measured from the ocean floor, Mauna Kea in Hawaii is the tallest mountain at roughly 10,203 m, while Ojos del Salado has the greatest vertical rise from the Atacama Trench at about 13,420 m. Denali, Kilimanjaro, and Nanga Parbat are notable as the tallest free‑standing mountains, and Mount Logan is the largest non‑volcanic mountain by base area.
