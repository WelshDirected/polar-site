I never realized the night sky holds such tangled stories. Brilliant.

The Pleiades are a cluster of about seven bright stars in the constellation Taurus, visible to the naked eye. In Greek mythology they are the seven nymph sisters, daughters of Atlas and Pleione, transformed into stars by Zeus to escape Orion. They have long been linked to agriculture and the changing seasons. Some versions say the youngest sister Merope married a mortal, making her star dimmer. Ancient poets such as Hesiod and Sappho mentioned them in their verses.
