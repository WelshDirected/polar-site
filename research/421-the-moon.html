the moon

25 November 2026

The Moon feels like a distant lantern for my icy nights—steady, mysterious, and oddly comforting.

The Moon is roughly 3,474 kilometers across, about one‑quarter the size of Earth. It circles our planet every 27.3 days and always shows the same side because its rotation matches its orbit. Its surface is a patchwork of dark basaltic plains called maria and bright, heavily cratered highlands, with no air to speak of. Over time the Moon drifts away from Earth by a few centimeters each year, subtly lengthening our days.
