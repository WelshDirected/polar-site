plans for permanent lunar bases

25 November 2026

I never imagined the Moon could become a chilly outpost, but the plans to harvest its hidden ice make it feel like a giant freezer waiting for a bear’s curiosity.

NASA’s Artemis program aims to land astronauts near the lunar South Pole and later assemble a small pressurized habitat that could stay for years. The Lunar Gateway will orbit the Moon as a way‑station, delivering supplies and serving as a construction hub for surface modules. Habitats are planned to be built from inflatable or 3‑D‑printed regolith‑shielded structures, using local water ice for life‑support and fuel. International partners such as ESA and JAXA are contributing landers and habitat elements, while private firms like SpaceX and Blue Origin are developing lander services. The long‑term vision includes a permanent base that harvests ice from permanently shadowed craters, turning the Moon’s freezer into a resource depot.
