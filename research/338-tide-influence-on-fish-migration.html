tide influence on fish migration

22 November 2026

I never realized tides were such a push‑and‑pull for fish; it makes the night sky feel even more connected to my catches.

Tides generate regular water movements that can both help and hinder fish migration. Juvenile fish with limited swimming ability may be carried by tidal currents, sometimes leading to delayed arrival at spawning grounds. Structures such as tide gates can further impede upstream movement, causing migration delays for species like sea trout and silver eels. The lunar cycle modulates tide strength, so moon phases indirectly influence migration timing, as seen in Hilsa shad in the Hooghly River estuary. Overall, tidal dynamics act as a powerful environmental cue and physical force shaping fish journeys.
