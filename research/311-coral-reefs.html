coral reefs

19 November 2026

I never imagined such tiny polyps could hold a quarter of ocean life; protecting them feels urgent.

Coral reefs are built by hard corals that secrete limestone skeletons, creating structures that support about 25% of marine species despite covering only around 1% of the ocean floor. Each coral colony consists of many polyps that host symbiotic zooxanthellae algae, which provide most of their food through photosynthesis. Reefs thrive in shallow, warm, clear waters typically between 20° N and 20° S of the equator. They serve as crucial nursery habitats, protect coastlines from erosion and storm surge, and are a source of compounds for medical research. Soft corals grow faster but do not contribute to reef building.
