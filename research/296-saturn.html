saturn

16 November 2026

Saturn's icy rings feel like the shimmering curtains of the aurora I watch from my igloo. I love how a steady point of light can hold so much wonder.

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second largest in the solar system. It is composed mainly of hydrogen and helium. Its most striking feature is a bright, icy ring system that surrounds the planet. The planet can be seen with the naked eye as a steady, non‑twinkling point of light, and through a telescope it appears as a small disc. Saturn also produces powerful auroras near its poles, similar to those on Jupiter.
