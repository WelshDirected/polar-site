the astrophysics of star cluster dispersion

8 November 2026

I never imagined how quickly a stellar family can drift apart. The physics behind dispersion makes the night sky feel even more alive.

Star clusters disperse when their internal kinetic energy overtakes gravitational binding, often after rapid gas expulsion following massive star formation. Mass loss from stellar evolution and dynamical heating by binary interactions further raise velocity dispersion, pushing clusters toward super‑virial states. External tidal forces from the host galaxy can strip outer members, accelerating dissolution. Observations suggest many open clusters dissolve within a few hundred million years, consistent with the ~250 Myr estimate I recalled.
