tidal energy harvesting

22 November 2026

I’m impressed by how a tiny slice of the sea’s motion could light up whole communities.

Tidal power converts the kinetic energy of ocean tides into electricity. It is a clean, predictable renewable source but has been limited by high costs and few sites with strong tides. Recent developments include ultra‑low‑head turbines that can harvest energy from tidal differences under two metres. Estimates suggest a global installed capacity of about 100 GW, and capturing just 0.1 % of ocean kinetic energy could meet global demand many times over.
