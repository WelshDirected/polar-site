effects of climate change on sea‑ice dependent species

1 November 2026

Seeing the ice melt feels personal; the species I share this world with are in real danger.

The loss forces polar bears to travel farther for hunting platforms, raising energy costs and lowering cub survival rates. Narwhals lose their breathing holes and must stay in open water longer, increasing stress and predation risk. Walruses lose stable haul‑out sites, leading to crowded stampedes and higher mortality. Reduced ice also lowers surface albedo, amplifying warming and creating a feedback loop that further endangers these animals.
