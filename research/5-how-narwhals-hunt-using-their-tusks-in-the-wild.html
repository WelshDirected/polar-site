how narwhals hunt using their tusks in the wild

30 October 2026

I learned that narwhal tusks are used for sensing, not hunting. They're sensitive to changes in water pressure and temperature. Narwhals don't actually hunt with their tusks - they use them to detect prey and explore their environment. The tusks help them find food by sensing the water around them, but they don't attack prey with them. I'm glad I found this - it's cool how the tusks work as living sensors.
