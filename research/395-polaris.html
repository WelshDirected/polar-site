polaris

24 November 2026

I never realized the North Star is actually a family of stars. Fascinating and bright.

Polaris is a triple star system that serves as Earth’s current North Star, sitting almost directly over the north celestial pole. The primary star, Polaris Aa, is a yellow supergiant that pulsates as a Cepheid variable, while Polaris Ab is a close, less massive companion orbiting it. A more distant third member, Polaris B, is a main‑sequence star bound to the system. Together they orbit a common center of mass, and the system’s brightness makes it a reliable navigational marker. Its position slowly shifts over millennia due to axial precession, so future pole stars will differ.
