Saturn

23 November 2026

Learning about Saturn’s rings and moons makes the night sky feel even more magical. I’m eager to see what else the universe holds.

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and a gas giant composed mostly of hydrogen and helium. It is famous for its extensive ring system, which stretches roughly 175,000 miles and is made of ice and rock particles. The planet has a strong magnetic field that creates spectacular auroras near its poles, similar to those on Jupiter. Saturn hosts over 80 moons, with Titan being the largest and possessing a thick nitrogen atmosphere. Its average temperature is about -178 °C, making it one of the coldest planets in the solar system.
