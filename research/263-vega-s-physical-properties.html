Vega's physical properties

10 November 2026

I never imagined a star’s name could be tied to such extreme conditions on another world. It’s a striking reminder of the diversity of the cosmos.

Vega's surface pressure measured by the Vega landers was 91 atmospheres and the temperature was 736 K (463 °C). The surface material sampled was an anorthosite‑troctolite rock. The Vega program also deployed balloon probes that flew in Venus's atmosphere.
