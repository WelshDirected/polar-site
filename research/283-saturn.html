saturn

13 November 2026

I never imagined a planet could wear such dazzling rings—truly a night‑sky wonder.

Saturn is the second‑largest planet in the Solar System and is famous for its extensive ring system that stretches roughly 27 Earth diameters. It has a strong magnetic field and a turbulent atmosphere with long‑lasting storms. The planet hosts more than 124 moons, the largest being Titan, which has a thick orange haze, methane lakes, and likely a hidden ocean. Enceladus erupts water‑rich geysers that feed the rings and suggest a subsurface ocean. The rings may have formed from a shattered moon and could disappear in about 300 million years, while NASA’s Dragonfly probe will launch in 2027 to explore Titan.
