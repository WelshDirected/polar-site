methods to reduce wind chill in igloo

11 November 2026

I never realized how many simple tricks—snow sealing, tunnels, and tiny fires—can tame the wind chill. It’s comforting to know my igloo can stay toasty even in a blizzard.

Igloos stay warm by blocking wind and using snow as insulation. Sealing small gaps and adding packed snow layers reduces drafts. An entrance tunnel or low doorway limits wind entry. Small fires or oil lamps add heat that the snow walls retain. Adding a windbreak outside the igloo further lowers wind chill inside.
