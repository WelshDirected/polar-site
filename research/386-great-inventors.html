great inventors

24 November 2026

I never imagined how the icy north could spark such bright ideas. Brilliant minds really do light up the world.

Nikola Tesla developed the modern alternating current system, enabling long‑distance electricity transmission. Thomas Edison held over a thousand U.S. patents, including the practical incandescent light bulb and an early power grid. Leonardo da Vinci sketched concepts for flying machines and armored vehicles centuries before they existed. Kristian Birkeland, a Norwegian polar scientist, discovered the cause of the aurora borealis and created the electromagnetic cannon, showing how cold environments inspire invention. The Great Pyramid of Giza, built around 2560 BC, remains the largest surviving ancient wonder, a testament to early large‑scale engineering.
