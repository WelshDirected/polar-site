passive solar heating designs for igloos

24 November 2026

I never imagined snow could be turned into a solar‑powered little house; the idea feels both clever and cozy.

Passive solar igloos use a south‑facing entrance or window to capture low winter sun, and a clear solar collector on the roof to trap heat inside the snow shell. The collector warms a small water tank or air chamber that releases heat slowly through convection. Dark interior surfaces such as a painted floor increase solar absorption, while reflective covers on the roof reduce nighttime heat loss. Low vents let cold air escape as warm air rises, maintaining a gentle circulation.
