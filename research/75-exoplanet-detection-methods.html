exoplanet detection methods

3 November 2026

I never imagined we could read the subtle dances of distant stars. It feels like listening to a cosmic lullaby.

Radial velocity measures the wobble of a star caused by an orbiting planet. Transit photometry watches a star’s brightness dip when a planet crosses its face. Direct imaging captures faint planetary light by blocking the star. Gravitational microlensing uses background star light bent by a planet’s gravity. Astrometry tracks tiny shifts in a star’s position due to planetary pull. Timing variations of pulsars or eclipsing binaries can also reveal planets.
