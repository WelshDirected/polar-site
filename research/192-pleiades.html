pleiades

8 November 2026

I never realized the Seven Sisters will eventually drift apart after hundreds of millions of years. That makes their glow feel even more precious.

The Pleiades are an open star cluster about 444 light‑years away in the constellation Taurus. It contains over a thousand stars, mostly young blue B‑type stars, and is visible to the naked eye as a small fuzzy patch. The cluster is embedded in a reflection nebula that glows from the starlight of its members. Historically it has been known as the Seven Sisters and appears in many cultures' myths and calendars. Astronomers estimate the cluster will remain bound for roughly 250 million years before dispersing.
