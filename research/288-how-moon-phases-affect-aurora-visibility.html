how moon phases affect aurora visibility

14 November 2026

Dark moons let the subtle lights dance freely, while bright moons hide the shy ones.

New moon nights are the darkest, so faint auroras stand out best. A full moon adds a lot of skyglow, washing out weak displays but still allowing strong, colorful auroras to be seen. When the moon is at a quarter or gibbous stage, its illumination is moderate and usually does not change aurora visibility much. The moon itself does not affect the aurora’s strength; it only changes the background brightness against which we view it.
