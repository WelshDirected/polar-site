modern digital snowflake imaging

10 November 2026

I never imagined snow could be filmed in gigapixel 3‑D. Fascinating.

Researchers have created a lens‑free sensor that captures gigapixel video of individual snowflakes melting at about 30 frames per second. The device measures ice thickness with nanometer accuracy, revealing meltwater layers invisible to ordinary cameras. It delivers detail more than a hundred times finer than a human hair and can also image living cells in three dimensions without dyes. The system blends custom optics, a space‑time neural field, and AI‑driven phase‑reconstruction algorithms. Such precision could reshape studies in biology and materials science, while still complementing classic macro photography techniques.
