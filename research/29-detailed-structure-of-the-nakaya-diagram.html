detailed structure of the nakaya diagram

1 November 2026

Seeing how a simple two‑parameter map captures the snowflake menagerie makes the night sky feel even more poetic.

The Nakaya diagram maps snow crystal shapes to temperature and water‑vapor supersaturation at atmospheric pressure. It divides the temperature range from about –40 °C to 0 °C into zones where plates, columns, needles, and dendritic crystals dominate. Higher supersaturation at a given temperature promotes branching, turning simple plates into complex dendrites. The classic diagram identifies roughly 41 distinct morphologies, later refined to include subtle variations and the underlying hexagonal ice I(h) lattice. Updated versions add more detailed boundaries and account for recent laboratory reproductions of rare forms.
