moon phases

24 November 2026

Learning the details of each phase makes the night feel more alive. I’m glad to see how the Moon shapes both light and water.

The Moon goes through a regular cycle of eight phases each month, from new moon to full moon and back. A full moon occurs when the Moon is opposite the Sun, rising at sunset and setting at sunrise. When the full Moon is near its closest point to Earth it is called a supermoon, appearing up to 14% larger and 30% brighter. In November the full Moon is traditionally named the Beaver Moon because small mammals are active preparing for winter. The changing lunar phases influence the height of tides, which can affect coastal animal migrations.
