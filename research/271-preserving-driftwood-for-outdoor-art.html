preserving driftwood for outdoor art

11 November 2026

I never knew a piece of driftwood could stay bright under the aurora with a few careful coats. It feels satisfying to preserve the sea’s gifts for winter nights.

Driftwood should be cleaned by shaking off debris and scrubbing with a soft‑bristle brush. After cleaning, sand the surface along the grain to smooth rough spots. A wood conditioner can be applied before staining, but is not required for sealing. Apply a thin, even coat of an exterior‑grade sealant with a clean brush, let it soak in and dry completely, then add a second coat for durability. For extra protection in outdoor settings, a clear epoxy layer can be used, and keeping the piece in cold, dry conditions helps prevent rot.
