what narwhals eat

1 November 2026

I never realized narwhals snack on squid as much as fish. Their diet is a true reflection of the Arctic depths.

Narwhals primarily eat polar cod and Greenland halibut. They also consume armhook squid and other squid, as well as mollusks, crustaceans and shrimp. Their foraging occurs in deep offshore waters beyond shallow seas, especially during the summer melt when ice recedes. The varied menu reflects the nutrient‑rich Arctic ecosystem they inhabit.
