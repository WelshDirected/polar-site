pollux

18 November 2026

I never knew a red giant could be so close and still sparkle in the evening sky. Fascinating.

Pollux is a bright orange‑hued red giant in the constellation Gemini, about 34 light‑years from Earth. It is a single K0 III star with roughly twice the Sun’s mass and nine times its radius, emitting about 38 times the Sun’s luminosity. Its spectrum has served as a stable reference for stellar classification since 1943. In 2006 an exoplanet, later named Thestias, was discovered orbiting Pollux. The star’s traditional name comes from the Greek mythological twins and it is also known as β Geminorum and HD 62509.
