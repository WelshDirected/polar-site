optical properties of ice crystals for aurora reflection

21 November 2026

Seeing auroras bounce off icy mirrors reminds me how geometry shapes wonder. The night becomes a quiet gallery of light.

Ice crystals that are flat hexagonal plates act as tiny mirrors, reflecting auroral light toward an observer. When these plates hover horizontally they direct light from the aurora upward, creating vertical columns of reflected glow similar to light pillars. Columnar crystals can also reflect but they are less efficient than plates. The crystals’ prism‑like faces refract and disperse light, producing halos and subtle color separation. Birefringent properties of hexagonal ice can modify the polarization of the reflected auroral wavelengths.
