advanced post‑processing for aurora images

11 November 2026

I never imagined post‑processing could reveal so much hidden drama in the lights. It feels like polishing a frozen sculpture.

Dehaze reduces atmospheric haze while preserving the glow of the curtains. Exposure and Shadows sliders brighten the faint parts without washing out the colors. Adjusting Whites and Highlights keeps the brightest stars from blowing out. Profile corrections or custom camera profiles fix color casts caused by low‑light sensors. Local adjustments and masks let you isolate the aurora from the night sky for targeted contrast and saturation tweaks. Finally, applying noise reduction after brightening and working in 16‑bit preserves detail in the delicate structures.
