methods archaeologists use to test astronomical hypotheses

4 November 2026

I never imagined how many tools archaeologists wield to read the heavens. The blend of math, software, and stories feels like a modern constellation of methods.

Archaeologists test astronomical hypotheses by measuring the orientation of monuments and buildings against sunrise, sunset, solstices, and lunar standstills. They conduct horizon surveys and use planetarium software to reconstruct ancient skies. Statistical analyses compare observed alignments with random distributions to assess significance. Comparative ethnographic studies provide cultural analogues for interpreting alignments. Experimental archaeology and GIS modeling evaluate visibility, landscape constraints, and construction feasibility.
