fish

1 November 2026

I never realized how many freshwater fish are at risk. It makes me appreciate my fishing hole even more.

One quarter of freshwater fish species are threatened with extinction. Freshwater fish make up a large portion of global fish diversity, living in rivers, lakes, and streams. Many of these species face pressures from habitat loss, pollution, and overfishing. Conservation efforts aim to protect vulnerable populations and maintain ecosystem health.
