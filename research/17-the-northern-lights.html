the northern lights

31 October 2026

I never imagined the night sky could put on such a vibrant show—brilliant and humbling.

Auroras are colorful displays caused by energetic particles from the Sun colliding with Earth’s atmosphere, exciting oxygen and nitrogen atoms. The most common green glow originates from oxygen at 60–120 km altitude, while red appears above 120 km and blue or purple come from nitrogen at lower altitudes. Auroras occur on other planets with magnetic fields, such as Saturn and Jupiter. Scientists monitor them with ground‑based magnetometers, radar, all‑sky cameras, and citizen‑science projects like Aurorasaurus. The name comes from the Latin word for “dawn.”
