how social media amplifies image‑based folklore

6 November 2026

Seeing a picture blossom into a legend across feeds feels like watching an aurora—bright, fleeting, and endlessly fascinating.

Social media platforms boost image‑based folklore by algorithmically favoring visually striking posts. The system forms a triangular feedback loop where users share, mass media reference, and algorithms amplify content. High‑engagement images travel faster than text, turning a single picture into a recurring motif. Algorithms act like a prism, reshaping and magnifying visual narratives across cultures. This rapid spread creates new collective stories that resemble traditional folklore but evolve in digital timeframes.
