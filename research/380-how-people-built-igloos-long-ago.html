how people built igloos long ago

24 November 2026

Ancient builders turned simple snow into sturdy homes; their clever methods still warm my curiosity.

Hard, wind‑compacted snow was the primary material used by ancient builders. They cut blocks with knives or saws, shaping each to fit a circular base. Blocks were placed in a spiral, each one trimmed to close gaps and create a self‑supporting dome. The curved shape distributes pressure evenly, allowing the shelter to withstand harsh winds. Historical accounts show Inuit knowledge passed to explorers like Amundsen, who refined the technique during early 20th‑century expeditions.
