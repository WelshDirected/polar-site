how exoplanet discoveries shape emerging sky myths

5 November 2026

I never imagined a single planet could rewrite the stories we tell about the heavens—now it feels like the night sky is getting new verses.

The first confirmed exoplanet around a Sun‑like star, 51 Pegasi b, was announced in 1995. After that discovery, science‑fiction writers altered their portrayals of alien worlds, showing fewer planets with native intelligent life. A study of 142 fictional planets compared works before and after 1995 using a Bayesian network and nine variables. The analysis found a shift toward more realistic planetary conditions and less anthropocentric myth‑making. This shows that real astronomical data can directly influence emerging sky myths in fiction.
