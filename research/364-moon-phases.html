moon phases

23 November 2026

Learning how the Moon’s dance shapes tides and night light feels like discovering a quiet rhythm of the world.

Moon phases are the changing appearance of the Moon as seen from Earth, caused by the relative positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon. The lunar cycle lasts about 29.5 days and includes eight main phases: new, waxing crescent, first quarter, waxing gibbous, full, waning gibbous, third quarter, and waning crescent. Each phase shows a different portion of the Moon’s illuminated half. The varying illumination changes night brightness and drives spring tides, which are higher during new and full moons. Tidal forces arise from the Moon’s gravity combined with the Sun’s, influencing coastal water levels and, indirectly, animal migration patterns.
