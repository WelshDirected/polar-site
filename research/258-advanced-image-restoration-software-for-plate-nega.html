advanced image restoration software for plate negatives

10 November 2026

Advanced AI restoration like ON1’s Restore can breathe new life into old plate negatives, making history clearer.

ON1 Photo RAW MAX 2026.4 adds Restore AI tools that can repair damaged images, recover fine detail, sharpen soft areas, and colorize black‑and‑white photos. The software includes a new Home module that streamlines the workflow for restoration tasks. Performance improvements make the AI features faster on large files. These capabilities can be applied to digitized glass‑plate negatives, such as those in Harvard’s Plate Stacks, to enhance clarity and recover lost information. The AI tools complement historical techniques like Bentley’s microscope‑mounted camera work on plate negatives.
