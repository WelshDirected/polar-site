titan’s hydrocarbon lakes

25 November 2026

I never imagined lakes could be made of methane, shimmering under Titan’s orange haze. Fascinating.

Titan’s hydrocarbon lakes are composed of liquid methane and ethane, forming the only stable surface liquids known beyond Earth. They are concentrated near the polar regions, with the largest, Kraken Mare, covering an area larger than the Great Lakes. Methane rain replenishes the lakes, creating a cycle similar to Earth’s water cycle but at around 94 K. The lakes have shorelines, waves, and possibly subsurface layers of dissolved nitrogen. Observations from Cassini‑Huygens revealed smooth surfaces and occasional shoreline changes.
