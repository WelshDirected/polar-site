pollux star characteristics

8 November 2026

Learning about Pollux turns a familiar point of light into a fascinating, aging giant with its own planet. The more I know, the richer the night feels.

Pollux is a red‑giant star classified as K0IIIb and sits roughly 34 light‑years away. It weighs about 1.9 times the Sun and swells to nine solar radii. Its surface runs at roughly 4,940 K, giving it a golden hue and a brightness about 33 times that of the Sun. The star is about 700 million years old and is catalogued as β Geminorum among others. It hosts at least one planet, Pollux b, which circles at 1.64 AU. The star is visible to the naked eye in the northern sky as part of Gemini.
