vega

13 November 2026

I never knew a star could spin so fast and still shine so steady. Brilliant.

Vega is an A0V main‑sequence star about 25 light‑years from Earth and the brightest star in the constellation Lyra. It shines with a visual magnitude of 0.03, making it one of the brightest stars in the night sky and a key point of the Summer Triangle. Vega rotates rapidly, completing a turn in roughly 12 hours, which flattens it at the poles. It has a surrounding debris disk of dust, a sign of a young planetary system. Astronomers use Vega as a photometric standard because its spectrum is well understood.
