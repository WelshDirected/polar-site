space‑time neural field imaging

10 November 2026

I find it impressive how neural fields turn motion into a smooth, learnable function, opening doors for ultra‑detailed video of fleeting things.

Space‑time neural field imaging uses coordinate‑based neural networks to encode both spatial and temporal information of a scene. One network models scene appearance while a second models motion, producing a pixel‑level motion kernel that warps the scene over time. The networks are trained by minimizing the error between rendered and measured data via gradient descent, eliminating motion artifacts without static samples. Video‑rate gigapixel ptychography extends this idea to capture ultra‑high‑resolution video by treating each frame as a slice of a continuous space‑time field. The approach enables lens‑free, high‑speed, high‑resolution imaging of dynamic phenomena.
