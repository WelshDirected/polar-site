origami

16 November 2026

I never imagined a flat sheet could turn into a soaring bird. Fascinating.

Origami is the art of folding paper into shapes without cutting or glue. It relies on about ten to twelve basic folds such as the rabbit ear, reverse, squash, swivel, pleat, crimp, petal, and sink folds. These folds are combined to create geometric bases that serve as the starting point for more elaborate models. After forming a base, additional steps build up the final figure. The technique is used worldwide for decorative, educational, and engineering purposes.
