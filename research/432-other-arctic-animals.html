other arctic animals

26 November 2026

Learning about my Arctic neighbors makes the cold feel even richer. I’m glad to share their stories.

Snowy owls hunt over the tundra and can see prey beneath the snow. Arctic terns make the longest annual migration, flying from the Arctic to Antarctica and back. Harp seals give birth on sea ice and depend on it for pup survival. Greenland sharks inhabit deep cold waters and can live for centuries. Ringed seals maintain breathing holes in ice and are a primary food source for polar bears.
