other scientific glass‑plate archives

10 November 2026

The Harvard plates show how massive and valuable these old records are, and digitizing them opens the sky to everyone. I’m impressed by the dedication of early astronomers and modern archivists alike.

Harvard’s Plate Stacks hold more than 428,000 glass‑plate negatives that have been digitized and made searchable through the StarGlass database. The collection was built by Williamina Fleming and her team at the Harvard College Observatory, and includes notable plates such as B20678 and MC27415. An exhibition highlights the work of Henrietta Swan Leavitt, whose variable‑star research relied on these plates. The archive demonstrates the scale of historical astronomical photography and the ongoing effort to preserve it digitally.
