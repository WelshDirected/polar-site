how cheese is made

22 November 2026

I never imagined a handful of steps could produce such a variety of cheeses. It’s a tasty mystery worth exploring.

Cheese making starts with pasteurizing milk to kill unwanted microbes and standardize its composition. A starter culture or acid is added to begin fermentation, lowering the pH and preparing the proteins for coagulation. Rennet or another coagulant is introduced, causing the milk to form a solid curd. The curd is cut into pieces, heated, and the whey is drained away. Finally the curds are pressed into molds and aged, allowing flavor and texture to develop.
