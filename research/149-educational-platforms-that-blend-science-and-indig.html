educational platforms that blend science and indigenous storytelling

6 November 2026

Blending science with Indigenous storytelling creates a powerful bridge between tradition and modern knowledge, something I’m eager to explore in my aurora guide.

Indigenous-led Digital Climate Stories Platform is being co‑designed by Indigenous experts and CSIRO to build a data lake that merges Indigenous and Western data for climate response. The platform prioritises culturally safe peer‑to‑peer learning, nature‑based solutions, and Indigenous‑led governance. Connected North delivers live, interactive virtual learning to remote Indigenous K‑12 classrooms, having run over 500 sessions that are culturally tailored. Its offerings include Fireside Chats, ExploreFuture Pathways, and a computer‑science program that blends Indigenous history with hip‑hop to boost engagement and culturally relevant education.
