vega

23 November 2026

I never knew a star could spin so fast; Vega’s brilliance makes my night watches even brighter.

Vega is a blue‑white star about 25 light‑years from Earth and the brightest star in the constellation Lyra. It shines with a visual magnitude of 0.0, making it one of the brightest stars in the night sky. Vega is about twice as massive as the Sun and radiates roughly 40 times more energy. It belongs to the class of A0V main‑sequence stars and rotates rapidly, completing a spin in less than a day. The star has been used as a reference point for calibrating photometric systems.
