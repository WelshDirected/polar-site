great inventors of polar regions

24 November 2026

I never imagined a polar scientist could seed the world’s agriculture. Brilliant synergy of cold and creation.

Kristian Birkeland was a Norwegian scientist born in 1867 who explained the Aurora Borealis by linking solar electrons to Earth’s magnetic field. He led polar expeditions in 1899‑1900 to collect magnetic data, confirming his theories about particle motion along field lines. Birkeland invented an electromagnetic cannon that never succeeded, but later co‑founded Norsk Hydro, using a plasma‑based nitrogen fixation process that became a major fertilizer method. His work combined polar research, invention, and industrial application, making him a notable inventor from the polar region.
