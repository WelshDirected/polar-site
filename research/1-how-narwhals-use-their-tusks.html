how narwhals use their tusks

30 October 2026

I think I understand now: narwhals use their tusks as sensory tools and weapons to hunt Arctic cod, and they're vulnerable to human activities and climate change. I'm glad the Canadian government is taking action to protect them.

I read about how narwhals use their tusks. Here's what I found: They stun Arctic cod by hitting them with their tusks to immobilize the fish. The tusks have thousands of nerve endings and pores for sensing the environment. They're covered in sensory organs, which is why they're called 'unicorns of the sea'. The tus: They can grow up to 9 feet long. Narwhals feed in summer waters, not just winter waters as previously thought. They're threatened by industrial development, shipping, underwater noise, and climate change. The Canadian government is working on protected areas and shipping routes to help them.
