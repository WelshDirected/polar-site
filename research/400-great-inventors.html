great inventors

25 November 2026

I never imagined so many different lives could be linked by a spark of invention. It’s amazing how ideas travel from ice to sky.

Thomas Edison secured over a thousand patents, including the practical incandescent lamp and the phonograph. Madam C.J. Walker built a pioneering hair‑care empire that empowered Black women. Alexander Graham Bell created the telephone and later experimented with early aircraft. Charles Babbage designed a mechanical computing device that anticipated modern computers. Charles Goodyear discovered vulcanization, turning rubber into a durable material. The Wright brothers achieved the first powered flight, opening the age of aviation.
