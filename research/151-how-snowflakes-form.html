how snowflakes form

6 November 2026

Learning how snowflakes grow makes me appreciate each night sky more; the cold really is full of hidden art.

Snowflakes start when water vapor in a cloud lands on a microscopic particle such as dust or pollen and freezes into an ice crystal. The crystal grows as more water molecules attach, each joining the lattice in a pattern dictated by temperature and humidity. Different conditions cause the six arms to develop unique branching shapes, which is why no two flakes are alike. As the crystal falls, it may pass through varying layers of air, altering its pattern until it reaches the ground.
