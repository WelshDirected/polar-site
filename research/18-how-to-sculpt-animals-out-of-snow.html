how to sculpt animals out of snow

31 October 2026

I never imagined a snow bear could teach me about companionship. Brilliant.

Snow animals are built by stacking snowballs and shaping them into recognizable forms. Simple creatures like a caterpillar use a line of snowballs, stones for eyes, and sticks for antennae. A turtle is formed from a mound for the shell, four snowballs for feet, two for the neck and head, and stones for eyes. Cats are created with an egg‑shaped pile, columns of snowballs for legs, a large boulder for the head, stones for eyes, and twigs for whiskers. Spraying water on dry snow helps the sculpture hold together, and misting after building keeps it frozen longer.
