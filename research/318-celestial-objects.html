celestial objects

20 November 2026

Learning about celestial objects reminds me how vast and varied the universe is, and it makes my nightly watches feel like a small part of a grand story.

Celestial objects are natural physical entities that exist beyond Earth's atmosphere, including stars, planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and galaxies. Stars are massive plasma spheres that shine by fusing hydrogen into helium. Planets orbit stars and have cleared their neighborhoods of debris, while moons circle planets as natural satellites. Smaller bodies such as asteroids and comets are remnants of the early solar system, often following elongated orbits. Ancient peoples charted constellations and planetary motions, laying the groundwork for astronomy. Contemporary telescopes, spacecraft, and spectroscopy have revealed exoplanets, black holes, and the large‑scale structure of the universe.
