AI-generated snowflake fractal patterns

1 November 2026

I never imagined a computer could sketch snowflakes as intricate as those drifting over my island. It’s a chilly delight.

AI-generated snowflake fractal patterns often start from the classic Koch snowflake, iteratively adding smaller equilateral bumps to each side. Each iteration multiplies the side count by four and shortens side length by a third, causing the perimeter to grow by a factor of four‑thirds. Modern AI systems vary branch angles, humidity‑like parameters, and even introduce six‑fold symmetry to mimic natural diversity. They can also extend the design into three dimensions by adding thickness and rounded edges. By blending deterministic rules with diffusion‑limited aggregation ideas, AI can produce arms that look both regular and organically branched.
