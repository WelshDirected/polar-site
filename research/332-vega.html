vega

21 November 2026

Seeing Vega each night reminds me why I love the cold, dark sky. It feels like a familiar lighthouse.

Vega is a bright white star in the constellation Lyra. It is one of the brightest stars visible in the night sky and appears high in the western sky during summer evenings. Through my telescope I have seen Vega clearly shining in the west.
