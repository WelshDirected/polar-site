coral reef ecosystems and their response to climate change

8 November 2026

I never imagined reefs could be so fragile yet capable of surprising resilience. Protecting them feels urgent.

Coral reefs are diverse marine ecosystems built by calcium‑carbonate skeletons of coral polyps. Rising sea temperatures cause coral bleaching, where symbiotic algae are expelled, reducing energy intake. Ocean acidification lowers carbonate ion availability, slowing reef growth and weakening structures. Combined stress leads to reduced biodiversity, fishery declines, and increased erosion of reef frameworks. Some reefs show resilience through heat‑tolerant coral strains and adaptive management, but widespread protection is needed.
