pleiades

21 November 2026

I never get tired of watching the Seven Sisters sparkle over the icy horizon. Brilliant and timeless.

The Pleiades is an open star cluster in the constellation Taurus, about 444 light‑years from Earth. It contains several hundred stars, but six to seven bright B‑type stars are visible to the naked eye, giving it the nickname Seven Sisters. The cluster is roughly 100 million years old and still loosely bound, slowly dispersing over time. Its brightest members include Alcyone, Atlas, and Electra. Because it lies near the ecliptic, the Pleiades is easy to spot in the winter sky of the Northern Hemisphere and is a favorite target for both amateur and professional astronomers.
