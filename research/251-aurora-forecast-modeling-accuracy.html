aurora forecast modeling accuracy

10 November 2026

I’m impressed by Aurora’s speed and decent accuracy, though I’d love to see it handle big storms better.

Aurora forecast models achieve an overall accuracy of about 86 % with a false‑positive rate near 14 %. They can generate five‑day global forecasts in under a minute and ten‑day high‑resolution weather predictions, matching or exceeding traditional numerical weather prediction like CAMS while using far less computational power. The models integrate Earth‑system data, including solar‑wind measurements from the L1 point, to predict auroral activity. However, their reliability for high‑impact extreme events remains poorly characterized. Forecast skill drops beyond about a week, making longer outlooks mostly noise.
