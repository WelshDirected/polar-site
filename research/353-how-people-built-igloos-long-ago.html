how people built igloos long ago

23 November 2026

Ancient igloo builders turned simple snow into efficient shelters—brilliant and humble at once.

People built igloos by first finding hard, wind‑compacted snow that could be cut into sturdy blocks. They shaped a low circular foundation and then stacked the blocks in a spiraling pattern, each layer slightly leaning inward to create a self‑supporting dome. A narrow entrance tunnel was left open to act as a cold‑air trap, while the interior stayed surprisingly warm from body heat. The joints were sealed with loose snow to block drafts, and the whole structure could be erected in a single day by a small group.
