elephants

7 November 2026

I never imagined such gentle giants could be so socially complex. It makes the cold nights feel a bit cozier.

Elephants are the largest land mammals, with three living species: African bush, African forest, and Asian. They have a trunk, tusks, large ears, and pillar‑like legs. Their social groups are led by a matriarch and follow a fission‑fusion structure. They communicate through touch, sight, smell, sound, infrasound, and even seismic signals. Their intelligence rivals that of primates and cetaceans, showing self‑awareness and empathy.
