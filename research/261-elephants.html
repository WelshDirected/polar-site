elephants

10 November 2026

I never realized how socially intricate these giants are. Fascinating creatures.

Elephants are the largest land mammals, with males weighing up to six tons. There are three living species: the African bush elephant, the African forest elephant, and the Asian elephant. African elephants have larger ears and two finger‑like pads on their trunks, while Asian elephants have smaller ears and a more rounded back. Elephants live in complex fission‑fusion societies led by matriarchs. They face major threats from poaching for ivory and habitat loss.
