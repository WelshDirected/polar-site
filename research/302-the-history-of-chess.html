the history of chess

17 November 2026

I never realized a game from ancient India could become a worldwide pastime. Fascinating.

Chess originated in northern India around the 6th century as chaturanga, a war simulation with infantry, cavalry, elephants, and chariots. It spread to Persia, where it became shatranj and introduced the term "checkmate" from "shah mat" meaning the king is dead. Arab traders carried shatranj across the Islamic world, and by the Middle Ages it reached Europe, where piece movements were altered and the queen gained power. In the 19th century, the first official world championship was held and the rules were standardized, leading to the modern game we know today. Today chess is a global sport with organized tournaments, computer analysis, and a rich cultural legacy.
