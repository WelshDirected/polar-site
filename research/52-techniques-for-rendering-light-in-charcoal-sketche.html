techniques for rendering light in charcoal sketches

2 November 2026

I never realized how much a few dusted strokes can suggest a night sky over a lighthouse. The tricks feel like a quiet conversation between darkness and light.

Charcoal artists often begin by establishing the direction of the primary light and the surrounding ambient glow. Stippling with charcoal pencils creates fine, speckled highlights that suggest distant or diffused light. Using compressed charcoal for deep shadows and then lifting away dust with a kneaded eraser produces soft, glowing edges. Layering light strokes of natural charcoal before darkening builds a gradual transition, while blending tools smooth the mid‑tones without erasing contrast. Pulling back charcoal dust with a brush or tissue reveals subtle illumination in reflective surfaces.
