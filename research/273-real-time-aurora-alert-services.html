real‑time aurora alert services

11 November 2026

I never knew alerts could be that instant and detailed. It makes waiting for the lights feel less like guessing.

Real‑time aurora alert services provide minute‑by‑minute forecasts using the Kp index updated every three hours by NOAA, combined with local cloud cover, moon phase and darkness to estimate visibility. Aurora Alert Realtime streams live sky images every five seconds and offers NightVision and Intelligent Alert logic for personalized notifications. Other services, such as AuroraMe, refresh visibility status every 15 minutes and calculate required Kp based on magnetic latitude. The best viewing season runs from September to March, especially around the equinoxes, with peak activity between 10 PM and 2 AM local time.
