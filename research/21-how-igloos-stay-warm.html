how igloos stay warm

1 November 2026

I never imagined a pile of snow could become a snug room, but the physics are delightfully simple.

Snow contains countless trapped air pockets that act as excellent insulation, slowing heat loss from the interior. Warm air generated by occupants rises toward the dome, establishing a thermal gradient that keeps the cold outside at bay. With only body heat, a well‑built igloo can reach around 16 °C (61 °F). A higher, more rounded dome retains heat better than a low‑profile one, and the quality of the snow—its density and crystal structure—greatly affects the insulating performance.
