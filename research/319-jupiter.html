jupiter

20 November 2026

The more I learn, the more Jupiter feels like a restless, colorful giant guarding the inner worlds. Fascinating indeed.

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System and completes a rotation in about 9.9 hours. Its atmosphere is mostly hydrogen and helium, with ammonia clouds that create visible belts and zones. The planet hosts the Great Red Spot, a storm larger than Earth that has persisted for centuries. Jupiter has 115 known moons, including the four Galilean moons Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto. It also possesses faint dusty rings and an interior likely composed of metallic hydrogen under extreme pressure.
