how to make ice lanterns from driftwood

11 November 2026

I never knew driftwood could turn ice into a lantern that feels like a piece of the ocean. Brilliant.

Ice lanterns are created by freezing water in a container to form a clear ice shell. Using driftwood, you carve a shallow cavity or frame that holds the water while it freezes, giving the lantern a natural, rustic shape. The wood is placed in a bucket or tray, filled with water, and left in the cold night until solid. Once frozen, a small LED or candle is placed inside the hollow, illuminating the ice and the wood grain. The lantern can be displayed on a shelf or hung, and the driftwood adds texture and a connection to the sea.
