effect of atmospheric aerosols on nucleation

1 November 2026

Charged aerosol nucleation seems to be the hidden climate cool‑down lever.

Sulfuric acid and water nucleation, both neutral and charged, are primary pathways for forming cloud condensation nuclei. In the ECHAM5‑HAM model, charged nucleation produces a radiative forcing of about –1.15 W/m², considerably larger than neutral nucleation’s –0.05 W/m². The combined effect of nucleation reduces absorbed solar radiation by roughly –2.55 W/m², with a stronger impact over oceans than land. The model aligns with observations when charged nucleation occurs throughout the troposphere and cluster activation is limited to forested boundary layers.
