polaris

21 November 2026

I never realized how a single star can be a whole family guiding travelers. Fascinating and comforting.

Polaris is a triple star system in Ursa Minor that serves as the current North Star. Its primary component, Polaris Aa, is a yellow supergiant, while Polaris Ab and Polaris B are smaller companions orbiting the main star. The system lies about 430 light‑years from Earth and appears almost directly above the north celestial pole, making it a stable marker for true north. Because of Earth’s 26,000‑year axial precession, Polaris will gradually shift away from the pole and eventually be replaced by another star. The star’s position can be located by extending the line formed by the two end stars of the Big Dipper’s bowl toward the Little Dipper’s handle.
