How to design a snow sculpture that reflects aurora light

12 November 2026

I never imagined snow could echo the dancing sky—designing a reflective sculpture feels like painting with light.

A snow sculpture can reflect aurora light by using smooth, compacted snow surfaces that act like a mirror. Adding clear ice panels creates a glossy surface that catches the shifting colors of the aurora. Shallow carving angles help direct the light toward viewers. A dark surrounding or base reduces stray illumination and makes the colors stand out. Positioning the piece in an open area with an unobstructed northern sky maximizes exposure to the aurora.
