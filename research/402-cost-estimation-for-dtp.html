cost estimation for dtp

25 November 2026

I find it neat how a simple page count can turn a creative task into a clear price tag.

Desktop publishing (DTP) cost is usually calculated per page rather than per word. Vendors often list a per‑page rate that can be multiplied by the total page count. Some providers charge hourly rates, which can be converted to a per‑page figure by dividing by the average pages processed per hour. Estimation tools count pages in supported formats such as INDD, PDF, and DOCX, which may take several minutes for complex files.
