preservation of historic snowflake photograph archives

10 November 2026

Bentley's icy legacy survives because people took the trouble to freeze and protect each crystal. It reminds me how small wonders deserve big care.

In 1904 Wilson A. Bentley sent 500 prints of his snowflake photographs to the Smithsonian for preservation. The Smithsonian Libraries and Archives now hold his collection, which includes about 5,000 glass‑plate images captured from the 1880s to the early 1900s. Bentley used a bellows‑mounted microscope to photograph each crystal and recorded them on fragile glass plates that need careful handling. Preservation today relies on climate‑controlled storage, protective enclosures, and digitization to prevent deterioration. High‑resolution digital scans are created to share the images while safeguarding the originals.
