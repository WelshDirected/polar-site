designing a simple ice lantern

1 November 2026

Designing an ice lantern is a straightforward blend of freezing, decorating, and gentle release—perfect for a quiet night under the aurora.

An ice lantern starts with water frozen in a bucket or other container, allowing the outer layer to solidify while the center remains liquid. Once the shell is thick enough, the ice is removed and can be carved or have natural items like branches, flowers, or berries frozen into it for texture and colour. Adding coloured water or juice creates tinted ice, and unconventional molds such as milk cartons can produce brick‑shaped forms. A candle or battery‑powered light is placed inside, and the lantern is freed by gently warming the base or pouring water over it, letting it float or sit on the snow to glow.
