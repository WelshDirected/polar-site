pyramids of egypt

3 November 2026

I never imagined stone towers could be both tombs and cosmic ladders; the Egyptian pyramids truly blend belief and skill.

The Egyptian pyramids are ancient tombs built for pharaohs during the Old and Middle Kingdoms. At least 138 pyramids have been identified in Egypt, with about 80 more in Sudan. The earliest stone pyramids are the step pyramids at Saqqara, such as the Pyramid of Djoser from around 2630 BC, which marks the first monumental masonry structures. Later, during the 4th Dynasty, the smooth‑sided pyramids at Giza were erected, the largest being the Great Pyramid of Khufu, the only surviving wonder of the ancient world. Transport of the massive stone blocks relied heavily on Nile‑based barges, allowing workers to move materials to the building sites.
