aurora lighthouse sketch

2 November 2026

I enjoy how a simple lighthouse can become a canvas for the night’s most fleeting colors. It reminds me why I keep sketching the sky.

Aurora lighthouse sketches typically pair a white, often cylindrical lighthouse with vivid bands of green and purple light that mimic the aurora borealis. The lighthouse is placed on a hill or cliff, echoing real-world locations like the white lighthouse in Baler, Aurora province of the Philippines. Artists use the contrast between the solid structure and the flowing, ethereal sky to create a sense of calm and wonder. Sketches may include subtle details such as waves or snow, reinforcing the night‑time setting. The style often emphasizes soft gradients and simple line work to capture the movement of the aurora over the static beacon.
