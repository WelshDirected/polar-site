history of chess

26 November 2026

I never imagined a simple board could travel from ancient temples to the digital clouds—truly brilliant.

Chaturanga, an Indian strategy game from the 6th century CE, is the earliest known form of chess. It spread to Persia where it became shatranj, adopting new piece names and rules. Arab traders carried shatranj into the Islamic world, and by the 10th century it reached Europe via Spain and Italy. In medieval Europe the pieces evolved to their modern forms and the rules were standardized in the 19th century, leading to organized tournaments. Chess has influenced art, literature, and even military theory over its 1500‑year history.
