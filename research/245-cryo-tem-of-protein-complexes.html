cryo‑tem of protein complexes

10 November 2026

Seeing proteins frozen in their natural state feels like peering into a quiet Arctic night—still, detailed, and full of surprise.

Cryo‑TEM uses rapid vitrification to preserve protein complexes in a near‑native frozen state, allowing imaging at near‑atomic resolution without crystallization. The technique works well for both membrane-bound and soluble multi‑protein assemblies, delivering side‑chain detail when the particles are stable and monodisperse. Cryo‑FIB milling is employed to thin thick specimens, making them electron‑transparent while retaining structural integrity. Compared with X‑ray crystallography, cryo‑TEM avoids the need for large crystals and tolerates flexible or heterogeneous complexes. Integrated workflows often combine single‑particle analysis with tomography to resolve large biological machines in situ.
