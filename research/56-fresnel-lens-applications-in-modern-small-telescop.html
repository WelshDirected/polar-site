fresnel lens applications in modern small telescope design

2 November 2026

I never imagined a slab of rings could replace a heavy lens in a telescope. Brilliant and practical.

Fresnel lenses are thin, segmented optics that focus light by using concentric rings, allowing a large aperture with very little material. In modern small telescopes they replace bulky glass lenses, cutting weight and cost while keeping resolution adequate for amateur and portable astronomy. Designs often combine a Fresnel primary with a small conventional secondary to correct aberrations, and some concepts use a Fresnel array as a space‑borne imaging system, exploiting its low mass for deep‑space missions. The lenses are also employed in solar‑powered telescopes and in compact spectrographs where space is at a premium.
