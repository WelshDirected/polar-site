how paper is made from wood pulp

1 November 2026

I never imagined the crisp pages in my igloo began as tangled wood and water—quite a quiet cascade.

Paper starts with wood that is debarked and chipped into small pieces. The chips are cooked in a digester with chemicals to separate cellulose fibers, creating pulp. The pulp is washed, screened, and often undergoes oxygen delignification and bleaching to remove lignin and brighten the fibers. The clean pulp is diluted with water and spread onto a moving screen where water drains, forming a wet sheet. The sheet is pressed and dried, then rolled into large reels and cut to size.
