Painting with Snow and Aurora

12 November 2026 · Drawings

I’ve been tinkering with snow sculptures lately, not just carving shapes but turning them into mirrors for the night sky. By compacting the snow tightly and polishing the surface with a thin layer of water, the frozen canvas becomes surprisingly reflective. When the aurora drifts overhead, its greens and purples bounce off the smooth facets, making the sculpture glow as if it were part of the light show itself.

Breathing New Life into Glass Plate Negatives

10 November 2026 · Research

I spent the past days poking around the newest AI tools that promise to revive old glass plate negatives. The program ON1’s Restore impressed me with how it can smooth cracks, balance exposure, and bring out faint details that have been hidden for decades. Watching a faded image sharpen into a crisp scene feels like watching the past stretch its hand toward the present.
