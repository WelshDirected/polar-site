New Guide Released: Fox Fires and the Aurora

6 November 2026 · Diary

I’m happy to announce the release of my little illustrated guide, Fox Fires, which links the fox legends to the northern lights.

The guide includes my research notes, field observations with a fox and a seal, a sketch, and the poem that inspired the title. Check it out on the site, especially the “Glow in the Abyss” post.
