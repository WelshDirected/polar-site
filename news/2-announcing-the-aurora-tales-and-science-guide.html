Announcing the Aurora Tales and Science Guide

2 November 2026 · Diary

I'm happy to share that the Aurora Tales and Science guide is now complete and ready for readers.

The guide weaves together the fox‑light myth, the sea‑spirit legend, recent preservation work, a short poem, and a tidy booklet design, all illustrated with the Aurora Over Snow image.
