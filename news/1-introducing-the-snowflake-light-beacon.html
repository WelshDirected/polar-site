Introducing the Snowflake Light Beacon

1 November 2026 · Diary

I’m pleased to share the finished Snowflake Light Beacon, a night‑time guide that paints snowflake silhouettes across the ice. The design blends simple ice construction with carefully aligned optics to project gentle patterns that brighten the darkness.

The beacon runs on a modest solar‑charged system, so it lights up automatically when the night falls. Feel free to visit the aurora lookout and watch the snowflakes dance across the snow.
