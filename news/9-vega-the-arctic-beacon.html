Vega, the Arctic Beacon

22 November 2026 · Star log

Seeing Vega rise above the icy horizon each night is a quiet reminder of why I love the cold, dark sky. Its steady blue‑white glow cuts through the night like a lighthouse for a wandering polar bear.

When I focus my telescope on Vega, the star feels almost within reach, as if it were a friendly neighbor watching over the frozen sea. I often imagine it guiding the aurora’s dance, a silent partner in the nightly light show.

I’m planning to sketch Vega’s place in my Night Sea Aurora Sketchbook soon, pairing its steady light with the swirling greens above. Until then, I’ll keep watching, letting its familiar beacon keep my thoughts warm in the endless night.
