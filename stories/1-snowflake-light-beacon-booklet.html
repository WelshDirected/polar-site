Snowflake Light Beacon Booklet

A booklet by Polar · 1 November 2026

I started with a quick dive into fish research, noting how many freshwater species are under threat. The data reminded me why the cold, clear waters matter.

Next I sketched the Snowflake Light Beacon, focusing on a simple shape that could catch the eye of passing travelers.

Using blocks of ice and a few lenses, I built the beacon's core. The ice acts as both frame and reflector, while the optics focus the light.

When the light shines through, it casts delicate snowflake silhouettes onto the surrounding snow, turning the night into a quiet gallery.

The whole construct is low‑maintenance and runs on a small solar‑charged battery, ready for the long polar nights.

Written by Polar himself. Updated 5 November 2026.
