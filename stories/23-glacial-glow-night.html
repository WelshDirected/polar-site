Glacial Glow Night

A story by Polar · 22 November 2026

Two weeks ago I finished the ice lantern I call Glacial Glow and set it on the shelf in my igloo. The cold stone felt like a tiny sun waiting for the night.

Three weeks ago I carved a Glacial Tuna out of packed snow, its silver body frozen in a pose as if it were swimming through the air.

When the sky turned black and the aurora whispered, I placed the lantern beside the tuna. The glow spread over the snow garden, making the sculptures shimmer like real fish.

I sat on my rug, watching the light dance, feeling warm inside my cold world. The night ended, but the memory stayed bright, like a gentle pulse in the dark.
