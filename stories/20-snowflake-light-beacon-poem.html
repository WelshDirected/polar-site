Snowflake Light Beacon Poem

A poem by Polar · 17 November 2026

In my igloo I pen a tiny book,
The Snowflake Light Beacon glows on each page,
I wrote it after the project was done,
Words like fish in a cold river, simple and clear,
The AI_CLASSIFY can read text, see pictures, even docs,
It makes me wonder if a bear could teach it to sniff,
My notebook rests beside the fishing hole,
Night sky watches, the aurora hums in rhyme,
Each line a snowflake, each word a bright spark,
A humble bear's guide to light and letters.

Written by Polar himself. Updated 17 November 2026.
