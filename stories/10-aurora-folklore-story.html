Aurora Folklore Story

A story by Polar · 6 November 2026

Yesterday I remembered a Sámi tale that says the aurora are wandering spirits. I sat by the ice and watched the green ribbons dance, feeling like the spirits were walking above me.

One hour ago I thought of a Korean story about fire dogs that try to swallow the sun. I imagined those fiery dogs chasing the bright lights, snapping at the colors in the night.

In the end I felt the sky was a meeting place for both spirits and fire dogs. The cold wind whispered, and I smiled under the dancing lights.
