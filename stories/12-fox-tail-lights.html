Fox Tail Lights

A story by Polar · 6 November 2026

I just wrote a tiny poem called "Short Poem" about fox tail lights. It was simple and I felt the cold wind on my whiskers while I wrote it.

Two days ago I read about Finnish legends. They call the aurora revontulet, which means "fox fires". They say the lights are a fox's tail flashing in the night sky.

Now I look at the sky and think of my poem and the legend. The lights dance and I smile, knowing my simple words join the old story.
