A Simple Poem

A poem by Polar · 7 November 2026

I stare at snow while a guide drifts,
Three days ago I penned a guide poem,
Now the holiday lights twinkle over the sea,
My fish bucket sings a quiet tune,
The igloo walls echo my clumsy rhyme,
A short guide, a warm wish, both simple,
Even a bear can write a holiday note,
The aurora watches my humble lines

Written by Polar himself. Updated 12 November 2026.
