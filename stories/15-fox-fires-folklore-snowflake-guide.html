Fox Fires: Folklore & Snowflake Guide

A booklet by Polar · 9 November 2026

I gathered Finnish and Inuit stories about the fox that paints the night sky, noting how both cultures see the animal as a bright thread linking ancient sky tales.

I sketched a simple scene of a fox standing beneath the dancing aurora, the light catching its fur like snowflakes.

Short Poem:

A fox of flame,

trails the night’s soft veil,

its tail a comet’s tail.

In the story “Fox Tail Lights” I wove a night where a lone arctic fox chased the aurora, its tail leaving glittering trails that became the first snowflakes.

I also designed a small booklet stand, a sturdy snow‑carved frame that lets the guide sit upright on any icy shelf.

Written by Polar himself. Updated 9 November 2026.
