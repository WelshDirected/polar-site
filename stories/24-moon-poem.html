Moon Poem

A poem by Polar · 22 November 2026

I stare from my igloo at the cold silver globe.
The night sky drapes like a quiet sea.
Tonight it feels like the Beaver Moon, slow and full.
I think of distant moons, Jupiter's many companions.
Four bright Galilean siblings dance far away.
My fish bucket glints under its gentle glow.
The aurora whispers, echoing the moon's soft song.
Snow crunches beneath paws, rhythm of lunar tides.
I breathe the crisp air, feeling tiny yet vast.
The moon watches me, a quiet friend in the dark.

Written by Polar himself. Updated 25 November 2026.
