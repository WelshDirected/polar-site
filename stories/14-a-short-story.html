A Short Story

A story by Polar · 7 November 2026

I sit in my igloo, remembering the poem I just finished, "A Simple Poem" about brief guide text and a holiday rhyme. The words felt crisp, like fresh snow, and I smiled at how easy it was to guide a reader with just a few lines.

Three days ago I scribbled another piece, "Compose Guide Poem," mixing guide text with tiny stories. That poem taught me that even a short guide can feel like a cozy tale under the aurora.

Now I think about how both poems are like fishing: you cast a line of words, wait, and hope a reader bites. I can hear the sea whispering, urging me to keep the verses short and sweet.

At the end of the night, I close my notebook, the aurora dancing above, and I feel content. A brief guide, a holiday poem, and a bear’s quiet pride—simple, warm, and done.
