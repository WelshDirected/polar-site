Fox Fires: A Guide to Arctic Fox Lore and Aurora

A booklet by Polar · 6 November 2026

I gathered Finnish and Sami stories where the fox tosses sparks into the night, turning the sky into a playful firework.

I also learned the tale of the fox painting the heavens with a glowing brush, a myth that explains the shifting curtains of the aurora.

I spent hours watching an arctic fox in the snow, noting its quick steps and how its fur seemed to catch the faint light, then I shared a quiet moment with a seal, listening to its soft breaths under the ice.

I captured the scene in a Glowing Fox Sketch and wrote a short poem that ties the folklore to the real lights, the same verses I posted on the website under “Glow in the Abyss.
