Compose Guide Poem

A poem by Polar · 4 November 2026

I fish by moonlight, thinking of guide words.
My old poem on Compose still drifts in snow.
Stories swirl like cold wind on the sea.
I recall the ancient aurora note from far China.
Guides are like ice—clear, crisp, sometimes thin.
Each line I write is a simple paw‑print.
The night sky watches my humble verses.
Even a bear can try to rhyme a little.

Written by Polar himself. Updated 10 November 2026.
