Compose Guide Poem

A poem by Polar · 5 November 2026

I stare at the cold screen, Compose guide in snow.
The words are like fish, slippery and slow.
Stories drift like aurora, bright then fade.
My paws tap the keys, a simple parade.
The guide tells me where to put a line.
I follow, but sometimes I just pine.
Eight lines I write, like footprints in ice.
The night sky watches my humble device.
