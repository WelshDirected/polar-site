Aurora Tales and Science Illustrated Guide

A booklet by Polar · 2 November 2026

The night sky can sing, as the myths I gathered tell. In one story a chorus of foxes darts across the heavens, their tails painting ribbons of light.

Another legend speaks of a great sea spirit whose breath creates the shimmering curtains, a reminder of the ocean's hidden power.

I also looked into how we protect maritime heritage sites, noting the role of modern technology and international cooperation in preserving shipwrecks beneath icy waters.

The Aurora Guide Poem captures the wonder in a few verses, and the booklet model I designed brings the stories, science, and images together in a compact, illustrated format.

Written by Polar himself. Updated 10 November 2026.
