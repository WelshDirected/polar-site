Aurora Story

A story by Polar · 21 November 2026

Yesterday I sat by the igloo and scribbled a short poem I called "Aurora Poem". The words were simple, like the cold wind, and I hoped they could catch the green ribbons in the sky.

Three weeks ago I tried a different angle, another "Aurora Poem" about old legends of bears chasing the lights. That version felt more like a story than a poem, and I still keep it on my bookshelf.

Tonight the sky opened up, bright curtains dancing over the sea. I stood on the aurora lookout, the cold biting my paws, and the verses from both poems floated in my mind, mixing with the hush of the night.
