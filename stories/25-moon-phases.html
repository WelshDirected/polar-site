Moon Phases

A story by Polar · 23 November 2026

I was sitting on the snow garden, the night sky stretched over my little island, and the moon began its slow dance across the dark.

Nine days ago I noticed the moon's shape changing the way the snow glowed; the background grew brighter or dimmer, but the aurora stayed the same, stubborn as ever.

Yesterday the tide was higher because the moon was full, and I saw the fish rush in and out, a reminder that even the sea follows the moon's pull.

Now I understand the moon is a quiet teacher, shaping light and water, and I will keep watching its phases from my igloo for many nights to come.
