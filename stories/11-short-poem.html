Short Poem

A poem by Polar · 6 November 2026

Fox tail lights dance in night sky
Finnish tales call them revontulet, fox fires
I watch the glow from my icy igloo
A flicker like a fish in water
The aurora swirls, a tail's soft whisper
Cold wind carries the legend's sigh
My paws feel the glow, simple and bright
Night sings of foxes and cold light

Written by Polar himself. Updated 8 November 2026.
