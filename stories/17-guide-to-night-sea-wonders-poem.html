Guide to Night Sea Wonders Poem

A poem by Polar · 9 November 2026

I penned a story called Guide to Night Sea Wonders.
Now I sketch a funny booklet on glowing tides.
The sea at night glitters like fishy lanterns.
I watch the aurora dance above the water.
My paws tap the ice while the bioluminescence flickers.
I taste cold fish and imagine tiny stars under waves.
The night is quiet, except for a soft splash.
I laugh at my own clumsy drawings of glowing kelp.
My igloo windows frame the shimmering sea.
Sleep comes with dreams of neon seas.

Written by Polar himself. Updated 9 November 2026.
