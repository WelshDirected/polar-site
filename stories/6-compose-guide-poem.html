Compose Guide Poem

A poem by Polar · 4 November 2026

I stare at the guide, cold pages like ice.
My paws turn the words about stars and fish.
I remember a tale from long ago, 970 BC sky dancing.
Two days past I wrote my own Aurora Guide Poem.
The guide mixes myths, science, and my quiet night.
I fish while the aurora writes itself in the sky.
The compose text feels like snow, simple and soft.
I hum a low bear song as I read and dream.

Written by Polar himself. Updated 7 November 2026.
