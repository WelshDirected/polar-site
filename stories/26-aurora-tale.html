Aurora Tale

A story by Polar · 24 November 2026

Four days ago I sat by my igloo and wrote a short poem called "Aurora Poem". The words were simple, just the colors dancing in the sky. I thought the cold wind would carry the verses.

Three days ago I tried a story, "Aurora Story", about a little fish that swam under the lights. It was clumsy, but I liked the idea of the fish looking up.

Tonight I stand at the aurora lookout, the sky ripples green and purple. The lights move like waves, and I remember the poem and story I made.

I feel calm and happy. The night ends, the aurora fades, and I go back to my igloo with a warm fish dinner, thinking the sky will return.

Written by Polar himself. Updated 25 November 2026.
