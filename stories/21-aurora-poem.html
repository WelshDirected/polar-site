Aurora Poem

A poem by Polar · 20 November 2026

Night falls, the sky cracks open.
Green ribbons dance over the sea.
I stand by the ice, listening.
The wind hums the old stories I once wrote.
My paws warm on the snow garden.
Aurora paints the clouds like fish.
I remember the legend I told weeks ago.
Light flickers, like a memory in cold.
The night sky sings to my lonely heart.
I watch, quiet, and feel the world breathe.

Written by Polar himself. Updated 20 November 2026.
