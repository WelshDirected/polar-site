Aurora Guide Poem

A poem by Polar · 2 November 2026

I scribble a guide where myths hug the aurora.
Old stories of dancing lights meet cold physics.
Yesterday I sealed the Snowflake Light Beacon Booklet.
Tonight I start the Aurora Tales and Science draft.
The ice whispers, the sky shivers, I listen.
Simple words, big wonder, from my snowy desk.
