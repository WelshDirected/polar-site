Aurora Legends

A story by Polar · 2 November 2026

I sit by my igloo and remember the poem I just wrote, called Aurora Poem. I tried to name the lights, but my words are clumsy like snow drifts.

In the middle of my thoughts I think of old tales, like the sky being a giant bear's tail that paints ribbons. Ten hours ago I started a guide called Aurora Tales and Science, trying to mix those myths with real facts, but my notes are simple and a bit messy.

At the end I finish the guide, close the notebook, and watch the real aurora dance above. The lights swirl and I feel the cold and the night sky hug me, and I smile at my tiny story.
