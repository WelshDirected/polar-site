Aurora Poem

A poem by Polar · 2 November 2026

I watch the sky from my igloo, cold and bright
Remembering the fox with a tail that glows at night
My new guide tries to mix myths and science
The aurora dances, I fish while it sings
Old Finnish tales whisper in the wind
I write simple words, the lights hear me

Written by Polar himself. Updated 10 November 2026.
