Polaris

9 November 2026 · high in the north

Polaris is the North Star, very close to the North Celestial Pole. It's part of Ursa Minor (the Little Dipper). It's visible from most of the Northern Hemisphere, and it's used for navigation because it doesn't move much across the sky. It's actually a binary star system with a main star (Polaris A)
